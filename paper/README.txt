Archive and Lean sources: https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.24254845

To produce main.pdf, run pdflatex main.tex three times from the arXiv
source root containing main.tex, references.tex, sections/ and figures/.
Appendix H gives the entry points for
reconstructing the circuits, rate windows, encoders, decoders and exact
fidelity queries. The tested engine was MiKTeX-pdfTeX 4.23 (MiKTeX 25.12).

The construction algorithms are finite prescriptions in the paper.
The archive provides no programs other than the Lean library,
which checks the two results listed in Appendix J.

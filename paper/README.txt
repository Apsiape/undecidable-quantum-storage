Undecidable quantum storage with seven elementary gates
Seth Douglas and Nidhal Mghirbi
Version 2.0.0, November 2026

Build: python -B build.py [--build-dir NEW_OR_EMPTY_DIRECTORY].
Requires Python 3 and pdflatex with the packages loaded by main.tex.
The build runs pdflatex three times without shell escape and rejects warnings,
unresolved references and overfull or underfull boxes.

main.pdf is the compiled preprint (65 pages);
abstract.txt is the abstract as plain text. Section 9
(sections/exponential*.tex) adds Theorem B and Proposition 9.8 with their proofs; the appendices are
fold-controls.tex, folded.tex and coexistence.tex. The bibliography is
references.tex.

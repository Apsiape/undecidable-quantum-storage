\section{Exponential storage on the nonhalting windows}\label{sec:exponential}
The finite-torus ceiling in Section~\ref{sec:ceiling} arises because a
causal chart must continue across successive time diagonals. We instead
place a static chart in a finite square and attach a finite counter.
Two coordinates carry this square; four transverse coordinates carry a
closed toric code. The classical rule is protected in two of the latter
coordinates and copied in the other two.

\begin{theorem}[Detailed form of Theorem~\ref{thm:exponential}]\label{v:detailed}
For each \(\mathsf r\in\{\mathrm{WS},\mathrm Q\}\) there is a separately
compiled, computably specified, translation-invariant, finite-range,
time-periodic circuit on \(\Z^6\), with \(N_{\mathsf r}\) qubits per
site and elementary inventory
\(\{X,H,\mathrm{CNOT},\mathrm{Toffoli},R_0,D_Z,I\}\), of arity at most
three. Noise in realization \(\mathsf r\) acts after every elementary
layer on every factor, including idle, reset, controller, counter, sampling register, probe, port, work and mixed ancilla factors. There are computable pairwise disjoint
nonempty rational open intervals \(I_n^{\mathsf r}\), a rational
\(c_{\mathrm e}>0\) independent of \(n\) and the same for both circuits,
and total computable thresholds
\(L_{\mathrm e}^{\mathsf r}(n)\), with these properties on
\((\Z/L\Z)^6\), using fidelity \eqref{eq:fidelity}.
\begin{enumerate}
\item Total computable maps \(\cE^{\mathsf r}_{n,L}\) and
\(\cD^{\mathsf r}_{n,L,t}\), independent of \(\eps\) and of the halting
answer, satisfy
\[
 F(\cE^{\mathsf r}_{n,L},\cD^{\mathsf r}_{n,L,t};L,t,\eps)\ge15/16
 \quad\bigl(U(n)\uparrow,\ \eps\in I_n^{\mathsf r},\
 L\ge L_{\mathrm e}^{\mathsf r}(n),\ 0\le t\le2^{\lfloor c_{\mathrm e}L\rfloor}\bigr).
\]
The same encoder serves every indicated integer time and rate.
\item If \(U(n)\downarrow\), then for every \(\eta>0\) there is a finite
\(C^{\mathsf r}_{n,\eta}\), independent of \(L\) and of
\(\eps\in I_n^{\mathsf r}\), such that
\(F^*_{L,\eps}(t)\le1/2+\eta\) for every \(L\), every such rate and
every integer \(t\ge C^{\mathsf r}_{n,\eta}\). The supremum includes
arbitrary encodings into the entire physical algebra and arbitrary
initial controller, work, intersite and reference entanglement.
\end{enumerate}
The closures of the \(\mathrm{WS}\) windows lie in \((0,1)\); those of the \(\mathrm Q\) windows
lie in \((0,1/(2MK_{\rm samp}))\), where \(M,K_{\rm samp}\) are the fixed probe count and
sampling depth chosen below. For each fixed rational \(0<\eta<3/8\),
no total computable bound on \(C^{\mathsf r}_{n,\eta}\) as a function
of \(n\) exists, by Proposition~\ref{v:certification}(iii).
\end{theorem}

Proposition~\ref{v:certification} gives the lifetime upper bound and the finite-test certification for this circuit; the scalable-memory labels in Table~\ref{tab:results} are classified by Corollary~\ref{cor:window-undecidable}; the scope is in Section~\ref{intro:scope}. The windows decrease as \(4^{-n}/P_{\rm pad}\) in \(\mathrm{WS}\) and \(4^{-n}/(MK_{\rm samp})\) in Q. In the affine layout used here, four independent quantum translations preserve two independent controller coordinates, so rank--nullity forces at least six coordinates within this layout class.

Section~\ref{v:classical-rule} gives the imported static chart, the square and its counter, the censored core rule and property~\ref{v:structure}. Section~\ref{v:density-section} bounds the density of faults on all paths, and Section~\ref{v:parameters} gives the source law and parameter order. Sections~\ref{v:positive} and~\ref{v:forgetting} prove parts (i) and (ii), respectively. Section~\ref{v:wrapped-section} describes a separate loss mechanism. Section~\ref{v:cert-section} proves a coarse lifetime upper bound and the certification result.

\subsection{Static squares and a counter with arbitrary initial phase}\label{v:classical-rule}
\begin{proposition}[Static classical interface]\label{v:static-import}
For the static chart of \cite{DMNoise2026}, version~1.4.0, applied with the wrapped machine \(V\) of Section~\ref{sec:controller}, the canonical projected geometry at cutoff \(a=n+n_0+1\), its finite owners and routing tags are computable on signed integer coordinates. If \(U(n)\uparrow\), it has a legal static chart extension with that projection and cutoff, with no halt letter. If \(U(n)\downarrow\), every sufficiently large compatible chart patch contains a fully guarded wrong critical ride: a short depth-\((a-1)\) end flagged zero or a long depth-\(a\) end flagged one.
\end{proposition}
\par\noindent\emph{Proof idea.} Compute ownership and routing in the fixed substitution phase. These facts let the board-filling proof apply there and supply the legal extension needed by the encoder.

\begin{proof}
Use the canonical construction in the proof of \cite[Lemma~11.2]{DMNoise2026} with its cutoff converse. Its substitution seed \(t_*\) is fixed by the child
\((2,2)\) of \(\sigma^2\). With
\(q(z)=\lfloor(z-1)/4\rfloor\), \(d(z)=(z-1)\bmod4\), its projected
letters satisfy
\(A_0(z)=\sigma^2(A_0(q(z)))(d(z))\). Signed integer coordinates
iterate to the fixed seed; the phase is
\(\varpi=(-1/3,-1/3)\), \(\tau_0=(-2/3,-2/3)\).
All ranks are finite since \(3u+1\ne0\) for integer \(u\).
Every coordinate has a finite frame owner: at zero
\[
 (0-\tau_{0,i})\bmod4^{d+1}=(4^{d+1}+2)/3
 \in[4^d+1,3\cdot4^d],
\]
with margins growing to infinity. Any fixed integer perturbation
therefore lies inside such a frame at sufficiently large depth.
Walls are unbounded in both directions, so nearest-wall orientations
and nearest-stop event tags are computable. The direct check of the routing conditions (O1)--(E3) in the proof of \cite[Lemma~11.2]{DMNoise2026} uses these three properties.

The board argument of \cite[Lemma~3.3]{DMNoise2026} forces the computation in every phase. The nonhalting proof of \cite[Proposition~4.3]{DMNoise2026} invokes typicality for the routing conditions and the nesting of active sites in boards. At the fixed phase, the routing check and the active-site argument in the proof of \cite[Lemma~11.2]{DMNoise2026} replace those two inputs. Each coordinate has a finite owner. A site whose two coordinates are free
at the same depth lies in that depth's board; all other sites are
horizontal-only or vertical-only carriers, or walls. Thus every active
site belongs to its proper board even at this nontypical phase.
The all-phase board argument forces the initialized finite run on each
board. The cutoff converse of \cite[Lemma~3.2]{DMNoise2026} uses only
finite ranks. Fill each board by
its finite computation, its channels from its endpoints and empty
records elsewhere, as in the proof of \cite[Proposition~4.3]{DMNoise2026}. Large boards run
\(V(n+1)=U(n)\); small or failed scans reach their boundary or fail
without a halt. On nonhalting no board contains a halt letter.
This produces a global legal static chart with the specified projection.

The guarded wrong-ride alternative is \cite[Proposition~4.3 and Lemma~10.5]{DMNoise2026}. These are the static inputs used below; the fixed-phase chart extension, counter, boundary geometry, initialization search and quantum estimates are proved in this paper.
\end{proof}

Write physical coordinates as \((u,P)\in\Z^2\times\Z^4\). A profile
\(\mathsf{pr}\) is inactive, or active with phase
\(\mathsf A,\mathsf D,\mathsf B\) and a letter of the static chart of
\cite[Sections~3--4]{DMNoise2026}. First put its base constraints in a
fixed nearest-neighbour higher-block presentation; attach the following
geometry and dynamics afterwards. A base constraint is checked only
when its whole bounding box is active. Impose five finite constraints:
\begin{enumerate}
\item A \(2\times2\) square cannot have exactly three active corners.
\item Horizontal active pairs belong to
\(\mathsf{AA},\mathsf{AD},\mathsf{DB},\mathsf{BB}\). At an inactive west
neighbour the phase is not \(\mathsf B\); at an inactive east neighbour
it is not \(\mathsf A\).
\item At an inactive south neighbour the phase is \(\mathsf D\) if
the west neighbour is inactive, and \(\mathsf B\) otherwise. At an
inactive north neighbour it is \(\mathsf D\) if the east neighbour is
inactive, and \(\mathsf A\) otherwise.
\item A \(\mathsf D\) site with active north neighbour has a
\(\mathsf D\) northeast neighbour; transpose southwest for south.
\item On a fully active \(2\times2\) square the southwest and northeast
phases agree.
\end{enumerate}
Let \(V_{\mathsf{pr}}\) mark every active participant in a forbidden
local pattern. It is a fixed finite local Boolean function.

\begin{lemma}[Square geometry and counter]\label{v:cap}
Every valid active component is a finite square or a product of two
infinite integer intervals. A finite square of side \(N_{\rm cap}\)
has exactly \(N_{\rm cap}\) diagonal \(\mathsf D\) sites and locally
recognizable endpoints. On this diagonal set
\[
 b'_j=b_j\mathbin\oplus y_j,\qquad y'_0=1,\qquad
 y'_j=b_{j-1}y_{j-1}\quad(1\le j<N_{\rm cap}).
\]
Overflow is \(o=b_{N_{\rm cap}-1}y_{N_{\rm cap}-1}\). It occurs within
\(1+2^{N_{\rm cap}+1}\) rounds from every state. With all bits zero and
only \(y_0=1\), no overflow occurs before \(2^{N_{\rm cap}}-1\).
\end{lemma}
\par\noindent\emph{Proof idea.} The local square constraints force a finite active component to be a square. Along its diagonal, a binary counter with delayed carries still increases by one until overflow.

\begin{proof}
The first constraint fills the bounding rectangle of any finite active
nearest-neighbour path: a new boundary site propagates along its new
row or column by the three-corners rule. Thus a component is a product
of intervals. If its horizontal interval is finite, every row has one
\(\mathsf D\), in the order \(\mathsf A^*\mathsf D\mathsf B^*\). Its position advances
by one on each row, so the vertical interval is finite. The boundary
rules put this diagonal at the southwest and northeast corners, forcing
a square. A finite-height, infinite-width rectangle is impossible:
choose a northeast diagonal away from any finite side endpoint. Its
bottom phase is \(\mathsf B\), its top phase \(\mathsf A\), contrary to
the fifth constraint. Infinite components therefore contain arbitrarily
large square patches.

For the counter, after one round \(y_0=1\), and
\[
 V_{\rm ctr}=\sum_{j=0}^{N_{\rm cap}-1}2^j(b_j+y_j),\qquad
 V'_{\rm ctr}=V_{\rm ctr}+1-2^{N_{\rm cap}}o.
\]
This follows from \(b\oplus y=b+y-2by\). Its bounded range precludes
\(2^{N_{\rm cap}+1}\) successive increments without overflow.
The specified initial value is one; overflow requires a value at least
\(2^{N_{\rm cap}}\), proving the lower bound. The local rule uses
copies, XOR, AND and a fresh output for overflow, all realized by the
seven gates of Section~\ref{sec:compilation}.
\end{proof}

One round of the classical rule has five parts.

\emph{(a) Core rule.} The ordinary core word is bottom or \((\mathsf{pr},b,y,\mathsf d)\). Its deterministic Moore-neighbour rule \(F_{\rm cap}\) retains the centre profile, updates the counter using original diagonal endpoints, and sets
\[
 \mathsf d'=\mathsf d\lor V_{\mathsf{pr}}\lor o\lor
 \left[\operatorname{active}(\mathsf{pr})\land
 \bigvee_{w\sim u,\ \operatorname{active}(\mathsf{pr}(w))}\mathsf d(w)\right].
\]
Off the diagonal the new counter bits are zero. A bottom input makes the output bottom, while inactive vacuum is a genuine nonbottom word. The storage-enable bit is \(\pi=\operatorname{active}(\mathsf{pr})(1-\mathsf d)\), with \(\pi(\bot)=0\); hence \(\pi(F_{\rm cap})\le\pi(\text{centre})\). Death leaves the original profile in place, so it creates no new counter endpoint.

\emph{(b) Protection and deep queries.} Protect the core in \(P_1,P_2\), copying in \(P_3,P_4\). At each replica in the three boxes \eqref{eq:c-boxes}, evaluate \(F_{\rm cap}\) on its nine Moore inputs, and let \(G\) return the word receiving at least 512 of the 768 votes, or bottom; these votes have a unique possible winner. Each site stores two coordinates: the second is the ordinary proposal \(w_{\rm ord}=G(C_t)\), where \(C_t\) is the field of retained first core coordinates before the update at round \(t\), and the first is \(w_{\rm ord}\) or bottom, as decided by the censor in (d). With \(v=(58,58)\) and \(D_{\rm q}=4096\), compute from old first coordinates the two finite queries
\[
 Q_0=G^{D_{\rm q}}(C_t)(u,P_{12}-(D_{\rm q}-1)v),\quad
 Q_-=G^{D_{\rm q}}(C_t)(u-e_x,P_{12}+v-(D_{\rm q}-1)v).
\]
Here \(G^{D_{\rm q}}\) means virtual iteration of the ordinary core rule; it contains neither sampling registers nor the statistical censor. Its virtual levels are evaluated locally from one transport of old words.

\emph{(c) Sampling registers.} Each sampling-register index \(j\) at storage position \(w\) belongs to base \(w-je_y\) and copies from \(w-e_x+v\). It has two seven-bit fields: a value field with 126 codes for high, one for low and one for unknown, and an availability field with 126 codes for allowed, one for unavailable and one for unknown; availability treats its last two codes as unavailable. All active, origin, end, phase, flag and edge-guard predicates read only the profiles of \(Q_0,Q_-\) at that base. The register takes the first applicable update in this list:
\begin{enumerate}[label=\arabic*.]
\item a bottom required driver writes (unknown, allowed);
\item current vacuum writes (unknown, unavailable);
\item a genuine active origin writes (low, allowed);
\item predecessor vacuum, or a failed genuine active-edge guard, writes (unknown, unavailable);
\item otherwise both old fields are copied.
\end{enumerate}
In particular an origin takes precedence over an inactive predecessor.

\emph{(d) Censor.} At an endpoint, gather the provisional sampling-register outputs by local recomputation from old words. Put \(V_{\rm av}=\#\text{allowed}\), \(H_{\rm up}=\#\{\text{allowed}\land(\text{high}\lor\text{unknown})\}\) and \(H_{\rm lo}=\#\{\text{allowed}\land\text{high}\}\), an upper and a lower count of high lanes. The test is enabled when \(V_{\rm av}\ge\lceil B/2\rceil\); put \(s_{\rm vote}=\lceil3C_hB/4\rceil\). The new first coordinate is bottom if either query is bottom, or if, at an enabled active phase-zero end, either the flag is one and \(H_{\rm up}\ge s_{\rm vote}\) or the flag is zero and \(H_{\rm lo}<s_{\rm vote}\). Otherwise it is \(w_{\rm ord}\). The second coordinate always remains \(w_{\rm ord}\), so postprocessing never writes a different nonbottom core. Historical bottom widens uncertainty and permits a test; it is distinct from a true inactive boundary.

\emph{(e) Quantum processor.} At fixed \(u\) use the closed four-dimensional plaquette CSS processor
of Lemmas~\ref{q:sweep}, \ref{q:homology}--\ref{q:span} and the proof of
Lemma~\ref{x:exponential}. In each round, for the bit sector followed by the phase sector, one
Clifford extraction writes the syndrome into fresh records, the snapshot
\(\sigma_0\). Local Boolean processing on these records alone computes
400 reflected sweeps by
\(\sigma_{j+1}=\sigma_j+\partial P_j\) for \(0\le j<400\), where
\(P_j\) is the plaquette set that sweep \(j+1\) selects from
\(\sigma_j\), and stores \(P=P_0+\cdots+P_{399}\). Only then are the
plaquette flips of \(P\) applied. The syndrome present before sweep
\(j+1\) means \(\sigma_j\); no sweep reuses an earlier sweep's decisions.
The record processing has range at most 400 code cells.
Track both sector chains at the common boundaries of full rounds,
just before the bit-sector extraction. The catalogue \(\Lambda_t\)
includes the allowed insertions and erroneous uses throughout the full
round, as in Lemma~\ref{q:round-shadow}.
Quantum
gates keep \(u\) fixed. The storage-enable bit changes only the existing final local
erasure decision. Every retained quantum-capable factor, including
syndrome/work, ports, data-derived records and unused factors, crosses
this complete cut or is reset/discarded before reuse. Quantum spatial
work precedes the longer classical calculation; no later swap retrieves
discarded input.

\paragraph{Structural property of both compiled circuits.}
\begin{enumerate}[label=\textup{(S)},ref=\textup{(S)}]
\item\label{v:structure} Every gate's quantum-capable inputs and
outputs lie in one fibre \(\{u\}\times(\Z/L\Z)^4\); these factors
include data, syndrome, work, ports, all quantum-derived records and
unused factors. Autonomous metadata \(\mathcal M\) is initially
dephased, has only Boolean basis permutations, resets and dephasings
as its own gates, and is never a target of a gate with a
quantum-capable input. Metadata controls may arrive from other
fibres; conditional on its initially measured word, its history law
is independent of the quantum input. In particular, after the last elementary
layer of every round, independent exogenous coins decide, with probability \(\eps\), whether to replace each site in \(\mathrm{WS}\) or each wire in Q, including idle factors. The coins are independent across positions and rounds and of all physical inputs.
\end{enumerate}
The processor satisfies fibre confinement. The autonomy construction in Section~\ref{sec:compilation} and the dephased scratch and reset probes in Section~\ref{v:parameters} ensure the metadata clause at every elementary gate. The noise definition \eqref{eq:site-noise} and its Q analogue give the final clause. Property~\ref{v:structure} therefore holds for every input to the compiled circuit, including malformed metadata.

\section{One-dimensional construction and its classical inputs}\label{q1:model}
Sections~\ref{q1:model}--\ref{q1:effective} prove Theorem~\ref{q1:main} and Corollary~\ref{q1:memory}. This section fixes the noise convention, the two classical lanes and the inputs taken from \cite{DMNoise1D2026}. Section~\ref{q1:clock} bounds the failures of the structural lane R1, which supplies addresses and ages. Section~\ref{q1:compiler} uses them to run a concatenated quantum code and proves level reduction. Section~\ref{q1:selector} builds the rate-selecting lane R2 from exact noise-driven replacements and bounds the joint error field. Section~\ref{q1:parameters} fixes every constant in a noncircular order. Section~\ref{q1:storage} proves storage on nonhalting windows, Section~\ref{q1:forgetting} proves whole-register forgetting on halting windows, and Section~\ref{q1:effective} assembles the proofs. Table~\ref{tab:terms-ring} lists the terms used in these sections.

We use the elementary gate and noise model of Section~\ref{s:local-circuits}. A macro round is a fixed finite schedule of \(D_{\rm layer}\) elementary layers; the noise after every layer is
\[
 \bigotimes_{\text{all wires }q}\bigl((1-\eps)\operatorname{Id}_q+
 \eps\operatorname{Tr}_q(\,\cdot\,)I_q/2\bigr).
\]
Every idle and reset layer is included. Registers have finitely many named factors per site. Gates such as SWAP, CZ and a classically controlled real gate below denote fixed decompositions into the elementary operations of Section~\ref{s:local-circuits}. Old and new classical banks are distinct, dephased at round boundaries, with one-way copies into private controls. Each displacement uses a distinct destination lane. Thus translated gates have distinct targets even when displacements coincide modulo a small ring length.

There are two autonomous classical lanes: a structural lane R1 initialized from a certified never-halting computation, and a rate-selecting lane R2. One round updates their whole native symbols, reads the old structural coordinates and retained word to execute one quantum instruction, performs the fixed port exchange, and uses the R2 strobe symbol to trigger terminal local erasure. Every nonretained quantum-capable factor is disposed of unconditionally after this erasure block. The autonomous banks remain dephased one-way controls; quantum gadget tapes and exchange ports are quantum-capable retained slots subject to erasure. Section~\ref{q1:forgetting} enumerates every factor.

We use the classical construction \cite{DMNoise1D2026} in the version and numbering listed in Appendix~\ref{app:imports-1d}.
Its Definition~\ref*{cl:def:sparse} fixes the sparsity convention.
Lemma~\ref*{cl:lem:sparse-witness} supplies the deterministic mesh
implication only; Lemma~\ref{q1:l1} replaces its iid probability bound.
For R1 we use the one-point serialization variant of
Section~\ref*{cl:sec:packets} proved in Lemma~\ref{q1:l9}: each bounded
scalar bit is moved from its scalar-bit tag to the low word bit of its
own live letter, with the other word bits zero; the typed word halves
and fixed endpoint padding are retained. Encoders, parsers and evaluators
all use that layout. Proposition~\ref*{cl:prop:organization},
Definition~\ref*{cl:def:observations} with its lookahead bound, and
Lemmas~\ref*{cl:lem:endpoint} and~\ref*{cl:lem:two-period} supply the
structural comparison under their stated protection and live-output
hypotheses. Lemma~\ref*{cl:lem:clean} identifies the clean full row,
and the proof of Lemma~\ref*{cl:lem:past} constructs its computable
aligned boundary. For R2, Lemmas~\ref*{cl:lem:stripes}
and~\ref*{cl:lem:receiver-mixing} supply tunable noise-only prefix tests
and all-input helper coalescence. Lemma~\ref*{cl:lem:locking} supplies
the literal lock, and Lemmas~\ref*{cl:lem:cap-cut}
and~\ref*{cl:lem:ascent} supply the arbitrary-start halting cap and
ascent. The initialized nested tests use the proof of
Lemma~\ref*{cl:lem:clock-witness}. The quantum probability bounds,
routing deletion, joint reduction and whole-channel argument are proved
below.

Fix a universal target machine \(U\). The R2 literal is the single fixed machine \(V\), with \(V(0)\) an infinite loop and \(V(m)\) simulating \(U(\nu_2(m))\) for \(m\ge1\). After fixing every gate and stencil choose the integer \(J_{\rm pad}\), and let \(m(n)=2^n(2J_{\rm pad}+1)\). The rate windows are rational subintervals of the monotone preimages of the native bins for \(m(n)\). Their construction never asks whether \(U(n)\) halts. The complete order of constants is in Section~\ref{q1:parameters}.

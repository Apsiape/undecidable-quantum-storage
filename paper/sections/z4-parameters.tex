\subsection{Order and bounds for the constants}\label{f4:constants}
All constants in Tables~\ref{f4:order-a}--\ref{f4:order-b} are fixed
for the medium, except the final window and size quantities. A finite
catalogue means the explicit port templates, provider offsets, typed
support/entry/corridor steps and source/use stars, with values of
alphabet words omitted. It can be enumerated by finite loops before
any truth table is materialized. The following convention makes the
bounds effective rather than an appeal to an unspecified finite maximum.
For such a catalogue \(\mathcal A\), let \(n(\mathcal A)\) be its
number of entries, \(R_x(\mathcal A),R_t(\mathcal A)\) the largest
absolute spatial and temporal offsets, and
\[
 V(\mathcal A)=(2R_x(\mathcal A)+1)^4(2R_t(\mathcal A)+1).
\]
A source affects at most \(n(\mathcal A)V(\mathcal A)\) labelled
uses, and a graph with finite edge catalogue has degree at most twice
its number of oriented entries. A doubled-tree description with marks
and cause choices is bounded by
\([8(\Delta+1)^2(K_0+1)]^{E+1}\). These deliberately generous bounds
are sufficient throughout; their numerical minima play no role.

For the negative path catalogue, include full provider-group contours,
their doubled spatial cubes and two time slices, and the actual
\(\sigma\)-edge/port/cut skeleton. Let \(\Delta_{\rm neg}\) be its
finite maximum degree, \(C_{\rm neg}\) its edge count per paid rare
or direct group, and \(R_{\rm neg}\) its maximum time increment.
These are obtained by the contour and slab construction in the proof
of Lemma~\ref{v:ancestor}, replacing its quantum-edge list by the
explicit dispatcher list. A crude recipe for \(C_{\rm neg}\) is to
multiply the ordinary contour coefficient by the total number of links
in a full source/group star, adding that star size once more; every
inserted link is then charged to its original paid group. Thus one may
use
\[
 K_{\rm rare}=[8(\Delta_{\rm neg}+1)^2]^{C_{\rm neg}+1},\qquad
 B_{\rm rare}=1+R_{\rm neg}(C_{\rm neg}+1).
\]
This counts connected marked descriptions and bounds their temporal
coverage. All entries use the fixed two-dimensional quorum and finite
quantum-contact template; none uses the number of code cells, the
calendar alphabet, helper count or padding length. Padding has no
quantum branching and is covered by its one round flag. The same
finite-star counting fixes \(K_{\rm hist}\) before \(B\), by
\eqref{v:history-count}.

Here the padding/cap row can be implemented without revisiting \(n_0\):
choose \(\eps_*\) rational with
\(\mu_*/2<1-(1-\eps_*)^{P_{\rm pad}}<\mu_*\), and initially put all
nominal windows below \(\mu_*/2\). Strict monotonicity makes this
rational search total. Thus no compiled hardware is chosen twice.
For clarity the helper tails used in Table~\ref{f4:order-b} are
\[
\begin{gathered}
 u_B=2^{-\lfloor3BC_h/100\rfloor},\quad
 z_B=2^{-\lfloor BC_h/3\rfloor},\quad
 w_B=2^{-\lfloor BC_h^2/50\rfloor},\quad
 b_B=2^{-\lfloor B(1/2-2C_h)^2\rfloor},\\
 \eta_+=3u_B/(1-4z_B)+2w_B+2b_B,\qquad
 \eta_-=4[u_B+w_B+2^{-\lfloor B(\rho_{\rm code}-1/2)^2\rfloor}].
\end{gathered}
\]
They vanish as \(B\) grows at fixed \(C_h\). The remaining strict
negative inequalities are
\[
\begin{aligned}
 K_{\rm hist}(2C_h)^{3/8}(2\mu_*)^{3/8}&<1/64,\\
 K_{\rm hist}[\eta_-+\xi_{\rm hist}^{1/D_{\rm ov}}]^{1/4}&<1/64,\\
 K_{\rm rare}[N_{\rm seed}(2\mu_*)^{256}]^{1/16}&<1/64,\\
 K_{\rm rare}(2\mu_*)^{1/2}&<1/64.
\end{aligned}
\]
Choose \(B\) and its overlay target for the second inequality before
shrinking \(\mu_*\); positive powers make all searches terminate.

Internal and padding whole-site flags \(E_{\rm int},E_{\rm pad}\)
are independent across actual sites/rounds, with
\[
 \nu=1-(1-\eps)^J,\qquad
 \mu=1-(1-\eps)^{P_{\rm pad}},\qquad
 p_H=\Pr(E_{\rm int}\lor E_{\rm pad})\le2\mu,\quad
 \nu\le2J\mu/P_{\rm pad}.
\]
The last padding replacement is a product uniform word, conditional
on its one flag. Thus helper marks are conditionally independent
uniform fields. Internal faults are arbitrary overlays; discrepancies
from the counterfactual clean sample are charged there. Padding adds
no quantum movement. This is the whole-site physical experiment used
in every source and negative estimate.

Finally, even with unit incidence factors, \(h_0\ge8\) gives
\(h\ge18\), \(a_{\rm sep}>79^2\), \(b_{\rm FT}\ge6240\).
The verified preparation in Lemma~\ref{d:gadgets} has at least
\(5^{2b_{\rm FT}}\) leaves. One qubit per virtual cell and one native
layer per round require \(P^5\ge5^{12480}\), hence
\(\alpha_4\le5/(12480\log_2 5)<2\cdot10^{-4}\).
The actual capacity and station multiplicity make it far smaller.

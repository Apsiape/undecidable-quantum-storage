\subsection{Order and bounds for the constants}\label{f4:constants}
All constants in Sections~\ref{f4:order-a} and~\ref{f4:order-b} are fixed
for the circuit, except the final window and size quantities. A finite
catalogue means the explicit port templates, replica offsets, typed
support/entry/corridor steps and source/use stars, with values of
alphabet words omitted. It can be enumerated by finite loops before
any truth table is materialized. The following convention makes the
bounds effective rather than an appeal to an unspecified finite maximum.
For such a catalogue \(\mathcal A\), let \(n(\mathcal A)\) be its
number of entries, \(R_x(\mathcal A),R_t(\mathcal A)\) the largest
absolute spatial and temporal offsets, and
\[
 V(\mathcal A)=(2R_x(\mathcal A)+1)^4(2R_t(\mathcal A)+1).
\]
A source affects at most \(n(\mathcal A)V(\mathcal A)\) labelled
uses, and a graph with finite edge catalogue has degree at most twice
its number of oriented entries. A doubled-tree description with marks
and cause choices is bounded by
\([8(\Delta+1)^2(K_0+1)]^{E+1}\). These bounds give the effective finite maxima used below; optimizing them is unnecessary.

\subsubsection{Geometric and circuit constants}\label{f4:order-a}
Each item ends with the quantities chosen after it.
\begin{enumerate}[label=\arabic*.,leftmargin=2em]
\item \textbf{\boldmath Local dispatch catalogue \(\mathcal A_{\rm loc}\).} At most 270 tuple templates,
730 ports; include cut/reset/idles, primitive decomposition and one-step
ownership stars. Physical operand diameter \(\le4\), marked radius \(\le8\).
\emph{Precedes.} All remaining rows.

\item \textbf{\boldmath\(h_{\rm base},\kappa_0,\kappa_1,v_0\).} Count footprints and incidences of
\(\mathcal A_{\rm loc}\). Use radius at least eight; the ownership part
is one child step. Bound counts by
\(732n(\mathcal A_{\rm loc})V(\mathcal A_{\rm loc})\), enlarging the
catalogue for each cover incidence.
\emph{Precedes.} Code, full normalizer length,
\(P_{\rm cal},T_{\rm cal}\), alphabet.

\item \textbf{\boldmath\(h_{\rm halo},\rho_{\rm gap},a_{\rm sep},b_{\rm FT}\).} \(h_{\rm halo}=2h_{\rm base}+2\),
\(\rho_{\rm gap}=1\) is allowed;
\[
 a_{\rm sep}=1+\lceil(4h_{\rm halo}+2\lceil\rho_{\rm gap}\rceil+5)^2\rceil;
\]
\(b_{\rm FT}=\max(1,\kappa_0\kappa_1v_0(a_{\rm sep}-1))\).
\emph{Precedes.} Code.

\item \textbf{\boldmath\(r_{\rm FT},s_{\rm code},q_{\rm code}\).}
\(r_{\rm FT}=8b_{\rm FT}\); least \(s_{\rm code}\) with
\(2^{s_{\rm code}}-1\ge40\,960(b_{\rm FT}+1)\).
\emph{Precedes.} Native table, \(P_{\rm cal},T_{\rm cal}\).

\item \textbf{Native table, \boldmath\(C_Q,P_{\rm cal},T_{\rm cal},r_{\rm exp},s_{\rm top}\).} Build finite real
gadgets; bound routed work by \(C_QP^2\). Search powers
\(P_{\rm cal}=2^{r_{\rm exp}}>2\) fitting all phases in \(T_{\rm cal}=P_{\rm cal}^4\). Choose
even \(s_{\rm top}\) exceeding complete spatial halos.
\emph{Precedes.} Source target,
sampling-register count, hardware depth.

\item \textbf{\boldmath\(\mathcal A_{\rm raw},K_0,c_0,R_{\rm phys},\Delta_{\rm raw}\).} Fixed
clock transport, old/new and use stencil; \[
 K_0\le n(\mathcal A_{\rm raw}),
\]
\(c_0\le n(\mathcal A_{\rm raw})V(\mathcal A_{\rm raw})\).
Take \(R_{\rm phys}\ge1\) to bound contour/source steps;
\mbox{\(\Delta_{\rm raw}\le2n(\mathcal A_{\rm raw})\)}.
\emph{Precedes.} Raw probability bound,
\(B,J\); independent of alphabet.

\item \textbf{\boldmath\(K_{\rm raw},C_{\rm w},K_{\rm w}\).} Tree ceiling above;
\(E<12c_0g\) plus enumerated attachment stars. Use its coefficient
for \(C_{\rm w}\), raise the tree ceiling to \(C_{\rm w}+1\) for
\(K_{\rm w}\).
\emph{Precedes.} Bound on \(p\).

\item \textbf{\boldmath\(R_{\rm pre},\Delta_{\rm pre}\).} \(D_{\rm q}=4096\),
\(R_{\rm pre}=2^{12}(D_{\rm q}+3)\);
\(\Delta_{\rm pre}=R_{\rm pre}^2[64(2D_{\rm q}+5)(9\cdot768+16)^2+256]\).
Enlarge once for fixed dispatch stars.
\emph{Precedes.} \(C_h,B\), palette.

\item \textbf{\boldmath\(\Omega_{\rm pre},L_{\rm star},c_C\).} Sum absolute three-potential offsets
on the source/entry stars (raw old/new contributes 696);
\(L_{\rm star}\) is their total link bound.
\[
 c_C=\lceil3(1440+\Omega_{\rm pre})/128+3+L_{\rm star}\rceil.
\]
Pole offsets are bounded directly by six times summed characteristic
coordinate offsets.
\emph{Precedes.} \(B\), palette.

\item \textbf{\boldmath\(R,K_{\rm cap}\).} Here \(R\) is the full increment bound of Lemma~\ref{f4:strip}. Union of preceding
catalogues with two formal roles and fixed gluing stars. Degree at most
sum of their degrees plus star incidence; size at most sum of size
bounds plus star lengths. In this row \(R_x\) bounds increments in
the chain-coordinate component \(\chi\), and \(R_\omega\) bounds
increments in \(\omega=x-t\) (Appendix~\ref{app:f4-sources}).
The image step is at most \(R_x+59R_\omega\).
Use the tree ceiling for \(K_{\rm cap}\).
\emph{Precedes.} Cutoff formula, source targets.

\item \textbf{\boldmath\(A_{\rm rec},d_0,B_{\rm tail},f_{\rm sh}\).} At most the square
of the number of children in the fixed \mbox{\(P_{\rm cal}\)-by-\(T_{\rm cal}\)} cover for
\(A_{\rm rec}\); root count has exponent five, enlarged for fixed
covers. Choose \mbox{\(f_{\rm sh}<\rho_{\rm gap}/(3\operatorname{Lip})\)},
where \(\operatorname{Lip}\) is the image increment of the preceding row.
Anchored enumeration fixes \(B_{\rm tail}\).
\emph{Precedes.} \(\gamma_{\rm eff}\) target.
\end{enumerate}

For the negative path catalogue, include full replica-group contours,
their doubled spatial cubes and two time slices, and the actual
\(\sigma\)-edge/port/cut skeleton. Let \(\Delta_{\rm neg}\) be its
finite maximum degree, \(C_{\rm neg}\) its edge count per paid rare
or direct group, and \(R_{\rm neg}\) its maximum time increment.
These are obtained by the contour and slab construction in the proof
of Lemma~\ref{v:ancestor}, replacing its quantum-edge list by the
explicit dispatcher list. A crude recipe for \(C_{\rm neg}\) is to
multiply the ordinary contour coefficient by the total number of links
in a full source/group star, adding that star size once more; every
inserted link is then charged to its original paid group. Thus one may
use
\[
 K_{\rm rare}=[8(\Delta_{\rm neg}+1)^2]^{C_{\rm neg}+1},\qquad
 B_{\rm rare}=1+R_{\rm neg}(C_{\rm neg}+1).
\]
This counts connected marked descriptions and bounds their temporal
coverage. All entries use the fixed two-dimensional quorum and finite
quantum-contact template; none uses the number of code cells, the
clock alphabet, sampling-register count or padding length. Padding has no
quantum branching and is covered by its one round flag. The same
finite-star counting fixes \(K_{\rm hist}\) before \(B\), by
\eqref{v:history-count}.

\subsubsection{Probability, hardware and window constants}\label{f4:order-b}
Each item ends with the quantities chosen after it.
\begin{enumerate}[label=\arabic*.,leftmargin=2em]
\item \textbf{\boldmath\(K_{\rm hist},K_{\rm rare},B_{\rm rare},N_{\rm seed}\).} Use the historical and negative catalogues above and set
\mbox{\(N_{\rm seed}=\tbinom{2\cdot352^2}{256}\)}.
\emph{Precedes.} Probability bounds, \(B\),
palette.

\item \textbf{\boldmath\(a_{\rm tail},D,\beta_{\rm cap},\beta_{\rm raw}\).} Use the two inequalities of Lemma~\ref{f4:recursion}. Set
\mbox{\(D=1/(2\max(1,A_{\rm rec}))\)}. Require the exceptional bases in
Lemmas~\ref{f4:raw} and~\ref{f4:cap-exception} strictly below one;
take any positive rational lower bound on their exponential rates.
\emph{Precedes.} \(C_h,B,\mu_*\).

\item \textbf{\boldmath\(C_h\).} Choose rational \(0<C_h\le1/100\) so
\(\sqrt{2C_h}\) is within the cap-source target.
\emph{Precedes.}~\(B\).

\item \textbf{\boldmath\(B=4^{k_B},\eta_+,\eta_-,\rho_{\rm code}\).} \(\rho_{\rm code}=126/128\);
search \(B\ge20/C_h\) making the tails \eqref{v:etas} sufficiently
small and \(4z_B<1\). Explicit tails are displayed below.
\emph{Precedes.} Support
colours, \(\mu_*,n_0\).

\item \textbf{\boldmath\(h_{\rm bin},h_{\rm hist}\), \(D_{\rm bin},D_{\rm ov}\),
\(\alpha_{\rm bin},\alpha_{\rm hist}\).} Enumerate full \(B\)-dependent physical
supports and overlap colours as in \eqref{v:joint}; choose positive
rational overlay targets for the remaining source/history margins.
\emph{Precedes.} \(\mu_*,J,P_{\rm pad}\).

\item \textbf{\boldmath\(\mu_*\).} Rational below \(C_h/100\), the positive, raw and density bounds
and all four strict negative inequalities below. Include the
raw bound \(382\,205\,952^{-67\,440}/128\).
\emph{Precedes.} \(n_0\), palette, hardware.

\item \textbf{\boldmath\(n_0\), palette, \(J\).} \(n_0\ge\max(k_B+2,3)\), all nominal windows
below \(\mu_*/2\). Build the finite chart/clock/compiler and count
its full internal elementary depth \(J\).
\emph{Precedes.} Padding, physical windows.

\item \textbf{\boldmath\(P_{\rm pad},\eps_*\).} \(P_{\rm pad}\ge2J\) with
\(2h_{\rm bin}JC_h/P_{\rm pad}\le\alpha_{\rm bin}\) and
\(2h_{\rm hist}JC_h/P_{\rm pad}\le\alpha_{\rm hist}\).
Choose rational \(\eps_*>0\) whose image under \(\mu\) lies in
\((\mu_*/2,\mu_*)\); nominal windows were put below \(\mu_*/2\).
\emph{Precedes.} Physical \(I_n\).

\item \textbf{\boldmath\(I_n,w_n,L_4(n)\).} Rational inner preimages of the nominal windows;
\(w_n=\lceil C_h/\mu(a_n)\rceil+2\) for \(I_n=(a_n,b_n)\).
Effective sufficient power bounds in the storage proof.
\emph{Precedes.} Initialization
and size analysis only; never circuit parameters.

\item \textbf{\boldmath\(\alpha_4,c_4\).} \(\alpha_4=1/r_{\rm exp}\);
choose positive rational \(c_4<(2s_{\rm top}P_{\rm cal})^{-\alpha_4}(\log2)/32\).
Enlarge the size threshold for fixed round depth.
\emph{Precedes.} Storage assertion;
independent of \(n\).
\end{enumerate}

Here the padding and rate-cap choice can be implemented without revisiting \(n_0\):
choose \(\eps_*\) rational with
\(\mu_*/2<1-(1-\eps_*)^{P_{\rm pad}}<\mu_*\), and initially put all
nominal windows below \(\mu_*/2\). Strict monotonicity makes this
rational search total. Thus no compiled hardware is chosen twice.
For clarity the sampling-register tails used above are
\[
\begin{gathered}
 u_B=2^{-\lfloor3BC_h/100\rfloor},\quad
 z_B=2^{-\lfloor BC_h/3\rfloor},\quad
 w_B=2^{-\lfloor BC_h^2/50\rfloor},\quad
 b_B=2^{-\lfloor B(1/2-2C_h)^2\rfloor},\\
 \eta_+=3u_B/(1-4z_B)+2w_B+2b_B,\qquad
 \eta_-=4[u_B+w_B+2^{-\lfloor B(\rho_{\rm code}-1/2)^2\rfloor}].
\end{gathered}
\]
They vanish as \(B\) grows at fixed \(C_h\). The remaining strict
negative inequalities are
\begin{align*}
 K_{\rm hist}(2C_h)^{3/8}(2\mu_*)^{3/8}&<1/64,\\
 K_{\rm hist}[\eta_-+\alpha_{\rm hist}^{1/D_{\rm ov}}]^{1/4}&<1/64,\\
 K_{\rm rare}[N_{\rm seed}(2\mu_*)^{256}]^{1/16}&<1/64,\\
 K_{\rm rare}(2\mu_*)^{1/2}&<1/64.
\end{align*}
Choose \(B\) and its overlay target for the second inequality before
shrinking \(\mu_*\); positive powers make all searches terminate.

The internal and padding fields \(A,E\) are independent, with independent flags at distinct physical sites and rounds. Their rates satisfy
\[
 \nu=1-(1-\eps)^J,\qquad
 \mu=1-(1-\eps)^{P_{\rm pad}},\qquad
 p=\Pr(A\lor E)\le2\mu,\quad
 \nu\le2J\mu/P_{\rm pad}.
\]
The last padding replacement is a product uniform word, conditional
on its one flag. Thus sampling-register marks are conditionally independent
uniform fields. Internal faults are arbitrary overlays; discrepancies
from the counterfactual clean sample are charged there. Padding adds
no quantum movement. This is the whole-site physical experiment used
in every source and negative estimate.

Finally, even with unit incidence factors, \(h_{\rm base}\ge8\) gives
\(h_{\rm halo}\ge18\), \(a_{\rm sep}>79^2\), \(b_{\rm FT}\ge6240\).
The verified preparation in Lemma~\ref{d:gadgets} has at least
\(5^{2b_{\rm FT}}\) leaves. One qubit per virtual cell and one native
layer per round require \(P_{\rm cal}^5\ge5^{12\,480}\), hence
\(\alpha_4\le5/(12\,480\log_2 5)<2\times10^{-4}\).
The actual capacity and station multiplicity make it far smaller.

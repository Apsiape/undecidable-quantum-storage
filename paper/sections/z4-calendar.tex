\section{Periodic clocks and raw sources on a torus}\label{app:f4-calendar}
The following extension of Section~\ref{d:calendar-section} supplies a
reference at every time, including times longer than any one clock
period. Write \(P_{\rm cal}=2^{r_{\rm exp}}\), \(T_{\rm cal}=P_{\rm cal}^4\).

Lemma~\ref{f4:raw} bounds raw clock faults on the torus, and Lemma~\ref{f4:roles} describes the two roles of the fold.

\begin{lemma}[Whole-state periodic clock]\label{f4:periodic}
For each fixed even \(s_{\rm top}\) and \(k\ge1\), a total finite
initialization on the cycle of length \(m=s_{\rm top}P_{\rm cal}^k\) implements the
repeated \(k\)-fold encoded identity, with aligned quantum periods
\(T_{\rm cal}^k\). Its complete classical state has temporal period dividing
\(T_{\rm cal}^{k+1}\). The virtual tower before the fixed fold decoration has initial spatial period dividing \(P_{\rm cal}^k\).
\end{lemma}
\par\noindent\emph{Proof idea.} Extend the interpreter so each simulation period returns its scratch records and port state to prescribed values. Build and verify this return using the fixed local phases.

\begin{proof}
Use the local interpreter of Section~\ref{d:calendar-section}, with
one additional requirement on its final phase: after committing the
next information and completing quantum-port use, return every
auxiliary field to its canonical age-zero value. The fields of one
virtual clock cell are specified in Table~\ref{f4:fields}. The
word ``blank'' denotes the fixed zero encoding of the corresponding
finite register; a head has the one declared start marker at address
zero and the no-head marker elsewhere. These conventions apply also
to deadline/default branches. The address and program are read-only.
A simulation-mode cell advances age modulo \(T_{\rm cal}\); an idle-mode cell
advances its age in the same way and blanks its irrelevant work.

The boundary/parity fields are fixed labels of the fold, and the
private copies are compiler work. Neither belongs to the simulated
virtual word; because both are constant or blank at every round boundary,
the identity below extends to the full physical clock. Inside a clean period, copy the three old strings, interpret one local
transition, broadcast its old instruction, execute its encoded table,
commit, and clean. Communication is \(O(P_{\rm cal})\), computation is polynomial
in \(\log P_{\rm cal}\), and the fixed routed table takes at most \(C_QP_{\rm cal}^2\)
steps. Canonicalization has the same local tape traversal bounds.
Thus a sufficiently large fixed power of two makes all phases fit in
\(T_{\rm cal}=P_{\rm cal}^4\). Define the revised total algorithm before writing its finite
description into the program field, as in Section~\ref{d:calendar-section}.

Let \(E_S(y)\) encode a cyclic word \(y\) in age-zero simulation
blocks with precisely these canonical fields, and let \(\mathcal C_S\)
be the clean logical transducer. Literal interpretation and the resets
give an identity on the \emph{entire} word,
\[
 \mathcal C_S^{T_{\rm cal}} E_S(y)=E_S(\mathcal C_S y).
\]
At the cyclic seam the same local neighbour communication applies as at
any block seam. Induction gives
\(\mathcal C_S^{T_{\rm cal}^k}E_S^k(y)=E_S^k(\mathcal C_Sy)\).
The uniform idle top word has clock period \(T_{\rm cal}\), spatial period one,
and identity instruction at every time. Its encoded word therefore
returns after \(T_{\rm cal}^{k+1}\), with initial spatial period dividing \(P_{\rm cal}^k\).
The exact port identity of Section~\ref{d:calendar-section} gives the
repeated encoded quantum identity in aligned periods \(T_{\rm cal}^k\).
The deterministic local evolution between returns defines all times.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Raw finite-torus cutoff]\label{f4:raw}
Consider \((\Z/L\Z)^d\) and a fixed collection of whole-symbol quorum covers using the
three \(16\)-by-\(16\) replica boxes of \eqref{eq:c-boxes}, with a
fixed finite logical stencil in the other coordinates. Before the
initial source slab the error reference is empty. Suppose one fresh
wrong site-round name has at most \(K_0\) raw causes, and the physical
site-time flags are independent, each of probability at most \(p\), and
one flag causes at most \(c_0\) such names.
Let \(R_{\rm phys}\) bound spatial infinity-steps of the complete
contour and source-star catalogue. Put
\(N_0=\lfloor(L-1)/R_{\rm phys}\rfloor\ge36\).
For a horizon \(T_{\rm hor}\), the event that some wrong use at a lifted
site-time up to \(T_{\rm hor}\) has a contour with at least \(N_0\) edges
has probability at most
\[
 C_{\rm raw}(T_{\rm hor}+C_{\rm raw})L^d
 K_{\rm raw}^{N_0+1}p^{N_0/(72c_0)},
\]
where \(C_{\rm raw}\) is a fixed root-count constant; for \(p\) below a
fixed bound this is at most
\(C_{\rm bd}(T_{\rm hor}+C_{\rm bd})L^de^{-\beta_{\rm raw}L}\).
Outside it every wrong use has a connected certificate with \(g\)
distinct physical raw names, at most \(C_{\rm w}g\) edges and at most
\(K_{\rm w}^g\) anchored descriptions. All probabilities are under
the original law.
\end{lemma}
\par\noindent\emph{Proof idea.} Apply the contour construction in a lift of the finite torus. Short graph pieces have distinct physical marks, which yields the raw cutoff after a union over their starts.

\begin{proof}
Lift the flags, the rule and its reference periodically from
\((\Z/L\Z)^d\) to \(\Z^d\). A replica at transverse displacement
\(s_{\rm rep}\) reads its logical inputs at \(u+s_{\rm rep}\), one round earlier;
the reference is cloned along the replica coordinates. Fix a realized
flag field and let \(y\) indicate that a lifted site-time carries its
reference whole symbol.

Define the typed dependence graph \((\Lambda,\mathcal H)\) of
\cite[Definition~20]{SST2026} on the lifted site-times from the round
before the initial source slab through \(T_{\rm hor}+O(1)\). Let \(\Lambda_1\)
be that first round, where \(y=1\). The three edge types are the three
pairs of replica boxes. At a site-time of \(\Lambda_\bullet\), type
\(s\) points to every logical input of every replica in pair \(s\).
Put in \(\Lambda_0\) the fresh wrong site-times, where \(y=0\) and
all the inputs in at least one pair have \(y=1\). All remaining
site-times are in \(\Lambda_\bullet\). Two correct boxes give \(512\)
correct votes, so every fresh wrong name has a raw cause in its update.

The associated monotone automaton \(\varphi\) is the OR over pairs of
the AND of their inputs \mbox{\cite[(2.14)]{SST2026}}. By the definition of \(\Lambda_0\),
\(y\ge\varphi(y)\) off \(\Lambda_0\), and on \(\Lambda_0\) the
automaton is fixed at zero. Finite time depth gives a unique trajectory
\(x\), hence the maximal trajectory of \cite[Lemma~13]{SST2026}. Induction from \(\Lambda_1\) gives \(x\le y\). At any wrong
lifted site-time \(i\) we therefore have \(x(i)=0\).
By \cite[Theorem~22]{SST2026} a Toom contour rooted at \(i\) is
present in this graph. Its sinks lie in \(\Lambda_0\). An edge
leaving the root or an internal vertex of charge \(s\) has type \(s\);
an edge leaving another source may have any allowed type
\cite[Definition~21]{SST2026}. The contour embeds in the finite explanation graph of \cite[Proposition~31 and Theorem~33]{SST2026}. The embedding is injective on sinks and on internal vertices of each charge by \cite[Definition~17]{SST2026}. Contour sources and sinks occur in equal numbers, so the contour is finite.
This construction is deterministic for each lifted field and does not
require independence between periodic copies.

Apply the zero-sum identity \cite[Lemma~41]{SST2026} in this spatial lift.
In the transverse coordinates \(u=(u_1,u_2)\) put
\(\chi=u+58t(1,1)\), with potentials
\(3(\chi_1+\chi_2),-3\chi_1,-3\chi_2\).
For the first potential use the two shifted boxes, and for the other two
use the unshifted box and the box shifted in the other direction.
Their gains are at least \(132,129,129\); the worst arbitrary backward
gain is \(-351\). Logical offsets do not enter these potentials.
Every dependence edge goes one round backward in time. Time telescoping
\cite[(5.13) in the proof of Lemma~38]{SST2026} gives the same edge
count \(j\) for each charge. The root is a contour source
\cite[Definitions~15--16]{SST2026}. Contour sources and sinks are equal in
number, so there are \(n_{\rm sink}-1\) nonroot sources for \(n_{\rm sink}\) sinks, each
emitting one edge of each charge \cite[(5.23) in the proof of Lemma~40]{SST2026}. Pole cancellation yields
\[
 0\ge3[129(j-n_{\rm sink}+1)-351(n_{\rm sink}-1)],\qquad
 E=3j\le(480/43)(n_{\rm sink}-1)<12(n_{\rm sink}-1)
\]
for \(n_{\rm sink}>1\); a single fresh root has zero edges.

For \(E\ge N_0\), root an abstract spanning tree at the contour root,
a source of weight zero. Give all \(n_{\rm sink}\) sinks weight one; their lifted
space-time positions are distinct by \mbox{\cite[Definition~17(i)]{SST2026}}. With at most
\(E<12(n_{\rm sink}-1)\) edges, its redundancy is below twelve. The separator
\mbox{\cite[Definition~2.4, Lemma~2.5 and Theorem~2 (Tree Separator)]{Gacs2021}} with
\(k_0=\lfloor N_0/12\rfloor<n_{\rm sink}-1\) gives a subtree with at most
\(12k_0\le N_0\) edges and more than \(k_0/3\ge N_0/72\) marked
original sinks. The separator's root convention assigns any removed
root mark to the other piece, so the selected marks remain original
sinks. Here \(N_0\ge36\) gives \(k_0\ge3\), so every refinement
inside Theorem~2 occurs at weight greater than three, as required by
its Lemma~2.5. Its spatial coordinate diameter is at most
\(R_{\rm phys}N_0\le L-1\). Two lifts of the same physical site-time
name would differ by an \(L\)-deck displacement in space; hence the
selected sink names are distinct physically. They require more than
\(N_0/(72c_0)\) distinct physical raw flags.

Truncate above \(T_{\rm hor}\) before extraction. The empty past forces every
vertex into the time slab from the initial round through \(T_{\rm hor}+O(1)\). There are at most
\(C_{\rm raw}(T_{\rm hor}+C_{\rm raw})L^d\) possible projected roots. Ordered
tree shapes, bounded edge increments, binary sink markings and at most
\(K_0\) causes per mark have at most \(K_{\rm raw}^{N_0+1}\)
descriptions. Apply the raw law only to admissible descriptions with
distinct physical images; all other codes merely overcount cardinality.
This proves the exceptional bound without assigning independent flags
to periodic copies.

Off that event a full explanation has fewer than \(N_0\) edges and
injective sinks by the same diameter bound. If \(g\) physical causes
occur, \(n_{\rm sink}\le c_0g\), so the contour and its fixed attachment
stars have \(O(g)\) edges. Bounded degree gives exponential anchored
entropy by traversing a spanning tree twice, recording finite edge
and role choices. All occurrences of a cause attach to its one raw
source label. Enlarging constants covers old/new time offsets and isolated roots.
For sufficiently small fixed \(p\), the exceptional bound is at most
\(C_{\rm bd}(T_{\rm hor}+C_{\rm bd})L^d e^{-\beta_{\rm raw}L}\).
\end{proof}

The next lemma is the one-dimensional two-sheet case of the fold
\eqref{q:fold-map}; Proposition~\ref{c:oriented-source} pays shared
physical faults across sheets in the same way.

\begin{lemma}[Two-role image]\label{f4:roles}
Fold a cycle of length \(m=2\ell\) into \([0,\ell)\) on a physical
circle of length \(L\ge4\ell\), using positions \(a_0(\sigma)=\sigma\)
and \(a_1(\sigma)=m-1-\sigma\) on that interval. Each extends to a
one-Lipschitz map into the virtual cycle. If distinct physical raw source labels have joint law \(p^{|F|}\),
duplicating each into two formal roles gives intersection weight at most
\((\sqrt p)^g\) for \(g\) distinct formal names.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
For \(\ell\ge2\) define \(f(\sigma)=\sigma\) on \([0,\ell-1]\),
\(f(\sigma)=2\ell-2-\sigma\) on \([\ell,2\ell-2]\), and zero
thereafter. Set \(a_0=f\), \(a_1=m-1-f\) modulo \(m\).
Every physical nearest-neighbour edge, including the torus seam,
changes \(f\) by at most one; the reflected role has the same property.
For \(\ell=1\) use constant \(f=0\). The map
\((\sigma,t)\mapsto(a_r(\sigma),t)\) consequently has bounded
increments on every fixed-range raw graph. At most two formal names
refer to one physical flag, so \(g\) names require at least
\(\lceil g/2\rceil\) independent flags. This proves the law.
All uses of one physical name in one role retain that name.
\end{proof}

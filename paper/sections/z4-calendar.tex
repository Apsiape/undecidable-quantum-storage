\section{Periodic calendars and raw sources on a torus}\label{app:f4-calendar}
The following extension of Section~\ref{d:calendar-section} supplies a
reference at every time, including times longer than any one calendar
period. Write \(P=2^{r_{\rm exp}}\), \(T=P^4\).

\begin{lemma}[Whole-state periodic calendar]\label{f4:periodic}
For each fixed even \(s_{\rm top}\) and \(k\ge1\), a total finite
initialization on the cycle of length \(m=s_{\rm top}P^k\) implements the
repeated \(k\)-fold encoded identity, with aligned quantum periods
\(T^k\). Its complete classical state has temporal period dividing
\(T^{k+1}\). The virtual tower before the fixed fold decoration has initial spatial period dividing \(P^k\).
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Use the local interpreter of Section~\ref{d:calendar-section}, with
one additional requirement on its final phase: after committing the
next information and completing quantum-port use, return every
auxiliary field to its canonical age-zero value. The fields of one
virtual calendar cell are specified in Table~\ref{f4:fields}. The
word ``blank'' denotes the fixed zero encoding of the corresponding
finite register; a head has the one declared start marker at address
zero and the no-head marker elsewhere. These conventions apply also
to deadline/default branches. The address and program are read-only.
A simulation-mode cell advances age modulo \(T\); an idle-mode cell
advances the same clock and blanks its irrelevant work.

\begin{table}[!htp]
\caption{Complete calendar state at age zero. Distributed fields have
one indicated bit or symbol per cell of a \(P\)-block.}\label{f4:fields}
\small\centering
\begin{tabular}{@{}p{.29\textwidth}p{.65\textwidth}@{}}
\toprule Field & Canonical value or retained information\\\midrule
Address & Its fixed residue modulo \(P\); zero in every top idle cell.\\
Age & Zero modulo \(T\).\\
Mode & Prescribed simulation or top idle mode.\\
Old information & The encoded virtual state string; blank padding.\\
Next information & Blank, after its contents have been committed.\\
Left, centre, right copy tapes & All blank.\\
Program & One bit of the fixed description of the total local algorithm;
blank padding; blank in every top idle cell.\\
TM tape & Blank auxiliary tape; read-only input/program roles retained
in their own fields.\\
TM head and state & Start state at address zero, no head elsewhere.\\
Broadcast gate field & Idle/default port labels.\\
Phase and handshake scratch & Blank; phase is determined by age.\\
Current native request & The scheduled age-zero instruction, here idle;
computed from age, address and broadcast fields.\\
Fold boundary and parity & Prescribed left/right/interior/outside flags
and virtual parity, retained by the logical rule.\\
Private old-word and selector copies & Blank at module boundaries;
reset before each use and after disposal.\\\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{table}

The boundary/parity fields are fixed labels of the fold, and the
private copies are compiler work. Neither belongs to the simulated
virtual word; because both are constant or blank at every round boundary,
the identity below extends to the full physical calendar. Inside a clean period, copy the three old strings, interpret one local
transition, broadcast its old instruction, execute its encoded table,
commit, and clean. Communication is \(O(P)\), computation is polynomial
in \(\log(P,T)\), and the fixed routed table takes at most \(C_QP^2\)
steps. Canonicalization has the same local tape traversal bounds.
Thus a sufficiently large fixed power of two makes all phases fit in
\(T=P^4\). Define the revised total algorithm before writing its finite
description into the program field, as in Section~\ref{d:calendar-section}.

Let \(E_S(y)\) encode a cyclic word \(y\) in age-zero simulation
blocks with precisely these canonical fields, and let \(\mathcal C_S\)
be the clean logical transducer. Literal interpretation and the resets
give an identity on the \emph{entire} word,
\[
 \mathcal C_S^T E_S(y)=E_S(\mathcal C_S y).
\]
At the cyclic seam the same local neighbor communication applies as at
any block seam. Induction gives
\(\mathcal C_S^{T^k}E_S^k(y)=E_S^k(\mathcal C_Sy)\).
The uniform idle top word has clock period \(T\), spatial period one,
and identity instruction at every time. Its encoded word therefore
returns after \(T^{k+1}\), with initial spatial period dividing \(P^k\).
The exact port identity of Section~\ref{d:calendar-section} gives the
repeated encoded quantum identity in aligned periods \(T^k\).
The deterministic local evolution between returns defines all times.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Raw finite-torus cutoff]\label{f4:raw}
Consider a fixed collection of whole-symbol quorum covers using the
three \(16\)-by-\(16\) provider boxes of \eqref{eq:c-boxes}, with a
fixed finite logical stencil in the other coordinates. Before the
initial source slab the error reference is empty. Suppose one fresh
wrong site-time name has at most \(K_0\) raw causes, and one actual
Bernoulli \(p\) site-time flag causes at most \(c_0\) such names.
Let \(R_{\rm phys}\) bound spatial infinity-steps of the complete
contour and source-star catalogue. Put
\(N_0=\lfloor(L-1)/R_{\rm phys}\rfloor\ge24\).
For a horizon \(H\), an exceptional event has probability at most
\[
 C_{\rm raw}(H+C_{\rm raw})L^d
 K_{\rm raw}^{N_0+1}p^{N_0/(72c_0)}.
\]
Outside it every wrong use has a connected certificate with \(g\)
distinct actual raw names, at most \(C_{\rm w}g\) edges and at most
\(K_{\rm w}^g\) anchored descriptions. All probabilities are under
the original law.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Write good as one minus the error indicator. Away from fresh sources,
the good cover is the OR over pairs of boxes of the AND of all required
logical inputs in that pair. This is an acyclic typed dependence graph.
The contour theorem and zero-sum identity of
\cite[Theorem~22 and Lemma~41]{SST2026} apply to each realized source
set, including its periodically correlated spatial lift.
In the transverse coordinates \(u=(u_1,u_2)\) put
\(\chi=u+58t(1,1)\), with poles
\(3(\chi_1+\chi_2),-3\chi_1,-3\chi_2\).
For the first pole use the two shifted boxes, and for the other two
use the unshifted box and the box shifted in the other direction.
Their gains are at least \(132,129,129\); the worst arbitrary backward
gain is \(-351\). Logical offsets do not enter these poles.
Every dependence edge goes one round backward in time. Time telescoping
gives the same edge count \(j\) for each charge. The root is a source
\cite[Definition~16]{SST2026}; for \(n\) sinks there are \(n-1\)
nonroot-source edges of each charge. Pole cancellation yields
\[
 0\ge3[129(j-n+1)-351(n-1)],\qquad
 E=3j\le(480/43)(n-1)<12(n-1)
\]
for \(n>1\); a single fresh root has zero edges.

For \(E\ge N_0\), root an abstract spanning tree at the contour root,
a source of weight zero. Give all \(n\) sinks weight one; their images
are distinct by \cite[Definition~17(i)]{SST2026}. With at most
\(E<12(n-1)\) edges, its redundancy is below twelve. The separator
\cite[Definition~2.11 and Theorem~2.14]{Gacs2021} with
\(k_0=\lfloor N_0/12\rfloor<n-1\) gives a subtree with at most
\(12k_0\le N_0\) edges and more than \(k_0/3\ge N_0/72\) marked
original sinks. The separator's root convention assigns any removed
root mark to the other piece, so the selected marks remain original
sinks. Its spatial coordinate diameter is at most
\(R_{\rm phys}N_0\le L-1\). Two lifts of the same physical site-time
name would differ by an \(L\)-deck displacement in space; hence the
selected sink names are distinct physically. They require more than
\(N_0/(72c_0)\) distinct actual raw flags.

Truncate above \(H\) before extraction. The empty past forces every
vertex into the initial-to-\(H+O(1)\) time slab. There are at most
\(C_{\rm raw}(H+C_{\rm raw})L^d\) possible projected roots. Ordered
tree shapes, bounded edge increments, binary sink markings and at most
\(K_0\) causes per mark have at most \(K_{\rm raw}^{N_0+1}\)
descriptions. Apply the raw law only to admissible descriptions with
distinct actual images; all other codes merely overcount cardinality.
This proves the exceptional bound without assigning independent flags
to periodic copies.

Off that event a full explanation has fewer than \(N_0\) edges and
injective sinks by the same diameter bound. If \(g\) actual causes
occur, \(n\le c_0g\), so the complete contour and its fixed attachment
stars have \(O(g)\) edges. Bounded degree gives exponential anchored
entropy by traversing a spanning tree twice, recording finite edge
and role choices. All occurrences of a cause attach to its one raw
hub. Enlarging constants covers old/new time offsets and isolated roots.
For sufficiently small fixed \(p\), the exceptional bound is at most
\(C(H+C)L^d e^{-\beta_{\rm raw}L}\).
\end{proof}

The next lemma is the one-dimensional two-sheet case of the fold
\eqref{q:fold-map}; Proposition~\ref{c:oriented-source} pays shared
physical faults across sheets in the same way.

\begin{lemma}[Two-role shadow]\label{f4:roles}
Fold a cycle of length \(m=2\ell\) into \([0,\ell)\) on a physical
circle of length \(L\ge4\ell\), using positions \(a_0(\sigma)=\sigma\)
and \(a_1(\sigma)=m-1-\sigma\) on that interval. Each extends to a
one-Lipschitz map into the virtual cycle. Duplicating each actual raw
hub into two formal roles gives intersection weight at most
\((\sqrt p)^g\) for \(g\) distinct formal names.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
For \(\ell\ge2\) define \(f(\sigma)=\sigma\) on \([0,\ell-1]\),
\(f(\sigma)=2\ell-2-\sigma\) on \([\ell,2\ell-2]\), and zero
thereafter. Set \(a_0=f\), \(a_1=m-1-f\) modulo \(m\).
Every physical nearest-neighbor edge, including the torus seam,
changes \(f\) by at most one; the reflected role has the same property.
For \(\ell=1\) use constant \(f=0\). The map
\((\sigma,t)\mapsto(a_r(\sigma),t)\) consequently has bounded
increments on every fixed-range raw graph. At most two formal names
refer to one physical flag, so \(g\) names require at least
\(\lceil g/2\rceil\) independent flags. This proves the law.
All uses of one actual name in one role retain that name.
\end{proof}

\section{Effectivity and the memory corollary}\label{q1:effective}
\begin{proof}[Proof of Theorem~\ref{q1:main}]
The finite circuit and its every-layer noise convention are specified in Section~\ref{q1:model} and Lemmas~\ref{q1:l2}, \ref{q1:l5}, \ref{q1:l10}, \ref{q1:l11}, \ref{q1:l14} and~\ref{q1:l17}. Their schedules have a fixed finite period and range, and commute with translation by one site. Lemma~\ref{q1:l16} chooses all constants without circular dependence, including the positive rational \(c_1=1/(12C_{\rm cone}T_q)\). Lemmas~\ref{q1:l1}--\ref{q1:l12} and the joint recursion of Section~\ref{q1:storage} give the hypotheses of Lemma~\ref{q1:l18}. Its finite chart works for every sufficiently large ring length and every intermediate elementary-layer time. It gives fidelity at least \(123/128>15/16\) after the initialization error. Lemma~\ref{q1:l15} makes both endpoint maps total on halting labels as well. Lemmas~\ref{q1:l13}--\ref{q1:l15} and the full-channel extension in Section~\ref{q1:forgetting} prove the size- and rate-uniform halting bound, including every small ring and every encoding into the whole register.

For completeness the certification assertion uses an exact finite calculation. Write \(f(n,L,t,q)\) for the Bell overlap of these particular endpoint maps. At fixed integer \(n,L,t\) it is a polynomial in the actual rate \(q\) with computable rational coefficients. Permutations, Hadamard conjugation, dephasing and reset have rational superoperator coefficients; the Bell density matrix is rational, the nominal endpoint mixtures have rational probabilities, and every physical replacement is affine in \(q\). This is the analogue of Proposition~\ref{a:rational} for these endpoint maps. Enumerate all \(L\ge L_1(n)\), all \(0\le t\le\lfloor c_1L\rfloor\), and all rationals \(q\in I_n\). Failure of \(f(n,L,t,q)\ge7/8\) is decidable for each triple, so failure of the universal predicate is recursively enumerable. Continuity gives the equivalent predicate over every real rate in the window. Nonhalting satisfies it. On a halting input choose \(\eta=1/16\), then take \(L\) sufficiently large that the uniform halting cutoff belongs to the tested horizon. Its rational midpoint fails the predicate. The predicate is therefore exactly \(\{n:U(n)\uparrow\}\), a \(\Pi^0_1\)-complete set.

A scalable-memory rate means \(\sup_L\tau_{L,\eps}=\infty\), as in Definition~\ref{def:windows}. The nonhalting lower bound gives this property at every rate of the window. In a halting window choose any \(\eta<3/8\); the uniform cutoff bounds \(\tau_{L,\eps}\) independently of \(L\) and \(\eps\). Thus scalable-memory labels are the same complete set. Hence an algorithm deciding scalability only at the computable rational midpoints of the windows would decide nonhalting. No classification of rates outside the listed windows is used.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{Lifetime ceiling.} The optimal lifetime of this circuit is at most exponential in \(L\) at every rate of every window. Let \(N_{\rm wire}\) be the number of qubits per site and \(e_{\mathrm{inf},m}>0\) the left endpoint of \(I_n\), with \(m=m_{\mathrm{bin}}(n)\) (Section~\ref{q1:parameters}). After each elementary layer the noise replaces all \(N_{\rm wire}L\) qubits of the ring by maximally mixed states with probability \(\eps^{N_{\rm wire}L}\ge e_{\mathrm{inf},m}^{N_{\rm wire}L}\), independently of the other layers. After such an event the register is independent of the reference, and every decoder gives Bell overlap \(1/4\). Hence
\[
 F^*_{L,\eps}(t)\le\tfrac14+\tfrac34\bigl(1-e_{\mathrm{inf},m}^{N_{\rm wire}L}\bigr)^{t}
\]
for every \(\eps\in I_n\) and every elementary-layer time \(t\), so \(\tau_{L,\eps}<e_{\mathrm{inf},m}^{-N_{\rm wire}L}=\exp(O_n(L))\) elementary layers. This is the bound in Table~\ref{tab:results}.

\begin{proof}[Proof of Corollary~\ref{q1:memory}]
Keep the structural lane R1 and its initialized certified nonhalting literal, the quantum slot inventory, the schedule of Lemma~\ref{q1:l10}, and the fixed quantum code. Replace every R2 operation and the conditional erasure block by identity layers on the same wires, so that the period, the gate range and every R1 and quantum stencil are unchanged. Dispose of every disposable factor as before. This gives another admissible circuit on rings, fixed, translation-invariant and periodic, in which every wire, including all retained control, is subject to the elementary single-qubit noise. The constants \(D_{\mathrm{layer}}\), \(R_{\mathrm{macro}}\), \(C_{\mathrm{cone}}\), \(T_q\) and \(C_{\mathrm{top}}\) of Section~\ref{q1:parameters} apply unchanged.

Its base error cover has only direct microscopic marks and the structural witness marks of Lemmas~\ref{q1:l1}--\ref{q1:l3}. Their primitive supports are bounded by the same enumerated stencils. For the integer moment exponent chosen in Section~\ref{q1:parameters}, direct moments tend to zero with \(\sqrt\eps\), and Lemma~\ref{q1:l1.1} gives a computable positive rational cutoff making the entire clock moment as small as required. Choose \(\eps_{\rm m}\) below these two cutoffs. The named-child geometry of Lemma~\ref{q1:l7}, the deletion lemma and the common correctable spans of Lemma~\ref{q1:l4} apply without change. The joint recursion of Section~\ref{q1:storage}, with the selector types absent, gives \(u_k\le b_*2^{-k}\) at every fixed \(0<\eps\le\eps_{\rm m}\).

Initialize the computable line boundary of Lemma~\ref{q1:l3.1} on the complete injective cone, recursively encode the nonport qubit, and fill the remaining ring as in Section~\ref{q1:storage}. No helper marginal is needed. Choose the largest fitting rank and complete an intermediate readout with nominal noise at any fixed rational rate in \((0,\eps_{\rm m}]\), followed by the same fixed recovery. Lemma~\ref{q1:l1} also covers the resulting rate splice because all its primitive key rates are bounded by \(\eps_{\rm m}\). The proof of Lemma~\ref{q1:l18}, with no initialization loss because no helper marginal is prepared, gives the linear elementary-layer horizon and fidelity at least \(1-1/32>15/16\) for all sufficiently large rings. The finite searches and recursive maps terminate, so these endpoints are total computable and independent of the actual rate. This proves the corollary with \(c_{\rm m}=c_1=1/(12C_{\rm cone}T_q)\).
\end{proof}

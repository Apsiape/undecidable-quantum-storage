\subsection{Halting-side fidelity for all registers}\label{f4:forgetting}
\begin{lemma}[Ancestry for the folded schedule]\label{f4:ancestor}
On a halting window, a specified physical initial site has retained
quantum ancestry at depth \(s\) with probability at most
\(A'_n(1+s)^{b'_{\rm c}}e^{-a'_ns}\) in an injectively unwrapped
determining cone. Here \(A'_n,a'_n>0\) and the fixed integer
\(b'_{\rm c}\) are uniform in the window
and simultaneously over every initial metadata word. All quantum
factors at that site are included in the same event.
\end{lemma}
\par\noindent\emph{Proof idea.} Apply the same halting obstruction to the static patch and count surviving paths using the actual folded dispatch edges. The final reset on each site's retained factors removes their dependence when storage is disabled.

\begin{proof}
The cap's negative argument is the one in Lemma~\ref{v:ancestor}.
We verify its graph hypotheses and the changed quantum path explicitly.
The static alternative there uses original profiles, active-neighbour
death ancestry and the deep-only base dictionary. Deleting copied
coordinates changes none of them. On halting, every arbitrary active
root is within bounded \(n\)-dependent original-active path distance
of a local violation, a fully guarded wrong critical motif, or a valid
uniformly bounded square. Finite-alphabet compactness covers malformed,
originless and wrapped components, exactly as in that proof. Persistent
or dead profiles remain distinct from vacuum, so cap boundaries and
counter endpoints are unchanged.

Full-core finite-age coherence and the all-path density bound of
Lemma~\ref{v:density} identify one immutable profile in a flag-only
good cone. The explicit active-neighbour death terms propagate ancestry
to the obstruction. A violation or arbitrary-phase expiry of a bounded
square contradicts late storage enablement. At a wrong motif a genuine origin
restarts the sampling lane and removes dependence on its initial contents. An earlier \(\bot\) writes an unknown value but leaves the lane eligible, so it cannot make either wrong critical test pass. A surviving sample pays
an intrinsic/overlay packet or a harmful nonbottom deep profile error.
The depth-4096 argument in Section~\ref{v:parameters} gives at least
sixteen distinct physical raw names per harmful query. Full-support slab
packing retains the last contour, including its part beyond
the sample prefix. Temporal coverage is
\(\lfloor t/K_{\rm age}\rfloor\le J_{\rm pack}(n)(f+m_{\rm pkt})\),
with \(J_{\rm pack}(n)=C_{\rm deep}+C_{\rm end}+8w_n+1\).
The same historical entropy, fixed before the sampling-register count, counts all possible profiles,
without conditioning on any initial word or chosen replica path.
H\"older's inequality between possibly overlapping families gives
\(p^{3f/4}\theta_{\rm pkt}^{m_{\rm pkt}/4}\), and
\[
 (2\mu)^{3f/4}(C_h/\mu)^{6f/16}
       =(2C_h)^{3f/8}(2\mu)^{3f/8}.
\]
The first two strict negative inequalities make the sum exponentially
small. Polynomial cone unions yield the all-start storage-enable and proposal
tail, uniformly in the window. Added clock metadata is autonomous
and contributes only to already counted internal overlays.

For centre ancestry at fixed \((x,y)\), use the minimal fields
(the local symbol \(\mathcal B_t\) here is a source field)
\[
 \mathcal B_t=H_t\lor H_{t+1},\quad
 \mathcal W_t=\mathcal B_t\lor\Theta_{512}(\mathcal W_{t-1}),\quad
 \mathcal N_t=\Theta_{512}(\mathcal W_t),
\]
started empty. A storage-enable bit still set at a late time outside that field would give a centre
path to the initial row; its late half is raw-marked by the preceding
tail, contrary to all-path density except for an exponential event.
A new \(\mathcal N\) birth needs 256 distinct fresh replicas or old
support in two boxes. The same two-dimensional quorum plane
\((\sigma,z)\) has four spatial parities and two time parities on
full doubled cubes of side 352. As in Lemma~\ref{v:ancestor}, for
\(g\) seed groups and \(k_{\rm dir}\) distinct direct names the joint weight is
\[
 (N_{\rm seed}p^{256})^{g/16}p^{k_{\rm dir}/2}.
\]
Full supports are reserved at every restart; complete rare contours
are attached to the quantum path.

For this schedule quantum edges move only a bounded distance in
\(\sigma\) or between finitely many on-site role/port/work factors.
They keep \(x,y,z\) fixed. All spatial quantum work precedes the
complete own-site conditional reset. A forbidden clean cut removes every
incoming quantum dependence; reset/preparation has no incoming edge,
and a discarded factor never returns. Thus every surviving path is a
connected description in the finite negative catalogue used to define
\(K_{\rm rare},B_{\rm rare}\). It has
\(g+k_{\rm dir}\ge s/B_{\rm rare}\) and at most
\(K_{\rm rare}^{g+k_{\rm dir}}\) descriptions. The last two negative inequalities
sum this tail exponentially. A polynomial determining-cone union proves
the lemma for a specified initial physical site, including both fold
roles and all work.

Every assertion averages the physical independent local whole-site
flags and their conditional product sampling-register marks. Arbitrary joint
quantum coefficients are an analysis of that physical circuit. Fresh
replacement/reset environments are never retrieved, so they supply
no returning path. Arbitrary initial programs, endpoints, counters and
clock words are included; clock synchronization is unnecessary
for this negative argument.
\end{proof}

\begin{proof}[Proof of Theorem~\ref{f4:detailed}(ii)]
Use Remark~\ref{rem:forgetting-interface} in dimension four. The autonomous, initially dephased cap and clock control the quantum circuit but never receive quantum-derived targets. Every quantum lifetime crosses its own-site cut or ends at reset/disposal, so Lemma~\ref{f:factorization} applies to the full initial-site mask. Lemma~\ref{f4:ancestor} supplies the uniform tail; each block has \(O(s^4)\) sites and determining-cone radius is \(O(s)\). On the finitely many smaller tori, full-register reset in one layer has probability at least \((\inf I_n)^{L^4}>0\). The shared extension, iteration and cloning argument therefore gives the claimed bound, after converting rounds to elementary layers. Its joint conditional histories retain all cap, clock and hardware correlations.
\end{proof}

For every label \(n\), every \(\eps\in I_n\) and every \(L\), the
full-register reset probability is at least \((\inf I_n)^{L^4}\)
in each layer. The argument of \eqref{eq:finite-reset} gives
\(F^*_{L,\eps}(t)\le 1/4+(3/4)(1-(\inf I_n)^{L^4})^t\), with \(t\)
counted in elementary layers. Thus the lifetime at fidelity \(7/8\) obeys
\begin{equation}\label{f4:lifetime-upper}
 \tau_{L,\eps}<\log(6/5)\cdot(\inf I_n)^{-L^4}+1=\exp(O_n(L^4))
 \quad\text{elementary layers}.
\end{equation}

For each fixed rational \(0<\eta<3/8\), these halting times have no total computable bound in the input \(n\). The argument of Corollary~\ref{a:time} applies with the computable threshold \(L_4(n)\) and the unbounded horizon \(\exp(c_4L^{\alpha_4})\): the finite circuits are computable, so approximating fidelity with absolute error less than half the gap between \(1/2+\eta\) and \(15/16\) and comparing with their midpoint would decide halting. No certification statement for this construction is added here.

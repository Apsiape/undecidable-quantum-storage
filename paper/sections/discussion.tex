\section{Discussion}\label{a:discussion}
The physical noise rate of a fixed seven-gate circuit can select between
exponentially long quantum storage and destruction after a time
independent of size. Theorem B proves this in six dimensions, with
separate circuits for whole-site and per-qubit noise. Theorem A retains
the sharp three-dimensional linear scale and exact promised
\(\Pi^0_1\) certification. Proposition~\ref{p:coexistence} retains a
four-dimensional single-qubit medium with a designed decidable
exponential window; its universal windows remain linear.

The computational charts and noise-window selector are published
ingredients of our earlier classical work
\cite{DMFixed2026,DMNoise2026,DMPhase2026}, in the tiling and
reliable-computation tradition of Robinson, Kari, Toom, Berman--Simon
and G\'acs \cite{Robinson1971,Kari1992,Toom1980,BermanSimon1988,Gacs2001}. Version 1 of this paper
\cite{DMQuantum2026} already supplied the seven-gate compiler, the
coherence, old/new density, slab-packing and H\"older methods of
Section~\ref{sec:controller}, common-recovery interfaces, the
full-channel sharing argument and the designed finite-torus exponential
estimate. Theorem B adds static capped
operation, a density law for names that seed two formal vertices, a total
static initializer, and complete correlated-source accounting on the
undecidable windows. Its finite counter avoids the causal-chart
ceiling while the original-active death rule retains the all-start
halting alternative.

The memory ingredients belong to an established literature: Ahn's
local higher-dimensional correction \cite{Ahn2004}, Kubica--Preskill's
sweep rule \cite{KubicaPreskill2019}, Harrington's local hierarchy
\cite{Harrington2004}, and G\'acs's reliable self-simulation
\cite{Gacs2001}, with transverse protection in G\'acs--Reif
\cite{GacsReif1988}. D\"unnweber--Styliaris--Trivedi already combine
G\'acs-style control with a measurement-free concatenated code
\cite{DST2026}. Related 2026 results include the local toric-code
automaton of Balasubramanian--Davydova--Lake \cite{BDL2026}, the
classical-to-quantum correspondence of Drechsler--Oreg--Aharonov
\cite{DOA2026}, and Ponarovsky's local sweep fault tolerance with
noiseless bounded-depth classical processing \cite{Ponarovsky2026}.
Selub--Bhardwaj--Lake give further local decoder context
\cite{SBL2026}. Thermal and dissipative memories address related
models \cite{Alicki2010,BDLin2026,KumarWeimer2026,PCC2011}; Hamiltonian
protection and space-overhead bounds concern complementary resources
\cite{PKSC2009,FMSh2022}. Undecidability of spectra, phase diagrams,
relaxation and channel properties precedes this operational question
\cite{CPGW2015,BCW1D2020,BCW2021,PLC2024,ShiraishiMatsumoto2021,WCPG2011,
EMG2012,KGE2014,DeLasCuevas2016,BMZ2026}.

The remaining questions include exponential storage on undecidable
windows below dimension six, undecidable storage in two dimensions,
a matching \(\exp(O_n(L))\) lifetime upper bound for the exponential
media, giving \(\exp(\Theta(L))\) lifetime on nonhalting windows.
Proposition~\ref{v:certification} gives the coarse
\(\exp(O_n(L^4))\) upper bound and exact \(\Pi^0_1\)-complete
certification. Smaller site dimension, nearest-neighbour
geometry and noise after every gate rather than after every layer also
require further constructions. The diagonal-band source cost of
Lemma~\ref{v:wrapped} leaves the continuing-noise wrapped-band
catastrophe hazard open.

\subsection*{Proof status}
The compiler, encoders, finite searches and constant choices are total
mathematical prescriptions; their tables and circuits were not
materialized, and the finite catalogue constants of
Section~\ref{sec:exponential} enter only through their finiteness and a
noncircular order of choice. The refutation-first checks of Theorem~B
re-derived the cap geometry and counter, the static and all-start
halting alternatives, Lemma~\ref{v:density}, the totality of the encoder
of Lemma~\ref{v:encoder}, the per-qubit layer, the alias-free extraction,
the order of choices, the windows and Lemma~\ref{v:wrapped}. They
checked against the text, without re-deriving, the encoder's success on
nonhalting inputs, the numerical catalogue constants, the group-contour
construction inside Lemma~\ref{v:bridge}, and the proofs of the
published results imported from \cite{DMNoise2026,SST2026} and from
Sections~3 and~6 and Appendices~B--C. There is no machine-verified proof.

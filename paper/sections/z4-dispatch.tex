\section{Exclusive folded dispatch}\label{app:f4-dispatch}
As in Appendix~\ref{sec:folded}, the folded semantic chain has two
qubits \(Q_{\sigma,0},Q_{\sigma,1}\) per physical site. Code coordinates, syndrome and Boolean quantum work use
additional chain cells. A requested native layer is a matching, padded
with one-qubit idles, of the real inventory \eqref{d:native}; its at
most three distinct operands are within two virtual edges of its first
operand. The line routing of Lemma~\ref{d:gadgets} supplies this form.

\subsection{A fixed private-port circuit}
This is the private-port circuit of Section~\ref{d:dispatch}, with
templates for the two fold roles and exclusive port selection.
For native type \(\tau\) of arity \(j\), enumerate templates
\[
 \theta=(\tau;(r_1,\delta_1),\ldots,(r_j,\delta_j)),\qquad
 r_i\in\{0,1\},\quad\delta_1=0,\quad\delta_i\in\{-2,-1,0,1,2\},
\]
with distinct role-offset pairs. Control/target order is part of the
type. An ordered tuple ID \((\theta,b)\) has operand \(i\) at
\(b+\delta_i\). Every template-position has a private port at every
site; at \(x\) it belongs only to tuple base \(x-\delta_i\).
There are at most
\[
 5\cdot2+3\cdot(4\cdot5)+8\cdot25=270
 \quad\hbox{templates},\qquad 10+120+600=730
 \quad\hbox{port roles}.
\]
The five unary types include reset, the three binary types are CNOT,
CZ and SWAP, and the ternary type is Toffoli. Repeated operands are
excluded. Tuple port sets are tensor-disjoint even on small tori,
because their internal labels differ. Their physical diameter is at
most four.

At each semantic vertex \(v=(x,r)\), a total Boolean function of
old dephased calendar requests and private neighbor copies chooses
NONE or exactly one eligible port at \(x\) with assigned role \(r\).
Resolve malformed metadata by a fixed priority. The two roles have
disjoint eligible lists. Reset ports, compute and freeze these one-hot
choices, scatter each semantic qubit to its selected port in fixed
serial order, apply every native tuple unconditionally, gather in
reverse order, and reset/discard ports and choice work at their declared
ends. Ineligible swaps are identity. One such reset, choice, scatter, native,
gather and reset sequence is a module.

The elementary implementation uses exactly the seven gates:
Fredkin is chronological CNOT\((b;a)\), Toffoli\((c,a;b)\),
CNOT\((b;a)\); SWAP is three CNOTs, and CZ is
\(H_{\rm target}\), CNOT, \(H_{\rm target}\). Boolean work uses private
reset, compute, copy and uncompute. Metadata is always a control of
quantum operations and never a quantum-derived target. Serialization
and private internal labels give a valid physical circuit on every
classical word. Each elementary layer, including all idle and
reset-following factors, receives the prescribed noise.

\begin{lemma}[Clean folded action]\label{f4:dispatch-clean}
The circuit implements an intended matching exactly on arbitrary
quantum data and a reference. A virtual cycle of length \(m=2\ell\),
\(\ell\ge3\), folds at fixed physical range into \([0,\ell)\).
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
In a clean matching, a tuple receives either all its intended semantic
operands or none. A wholly unused tuple acts only on disposable ports.
Scatter and gather therefore implement each intended channel exactly,
including reset: the reset acts on the port holding the intended input,
and its reset output is gathered before disposal. Dephasing, when
required on work, is handled in the same way, without inverting it.
The coordinate is \(a=\sigma\) on role zero and
\(a=m-1-\sigma\) on role one. Successor means physical right motion
on role zero, an onsite switch at the right endpoint, left motion on
role one, and an onsite switch at the left endpoint. Radius-two virtual
operands fit the catalogue. Fixed left/right/interior/outside flags,
including both endpoint flags when needed, determine the one- and
two-step neighbors locally. Virtual parity is \(\sigma\bmod2\) in
role zero and \(1-\sigma\bmod2\) in role one. Outside factors have
the declared reset/idle reference. Degenerate intervals use a total
reset fallback. Nothing in the rule reads the interval length.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Closed support and joint localization]\label{f4:dispatch}
Fix an intended matching. Let \(B_{\rm ch}\) be the clean-station
vertices whose actual exclusive tuple differs from their intended
tuple, \(D_{\rm st}\) the dirty physical stations, and \(E_{\rm tu}\)
the dirty native tuple boxes. A station fault may act jointly on its
two semantic qubits, all its ports and routing controls. Dirty boxes
may share operator or environment coefficients. There is a closed
union of intended locations, of cardinality at most
\[
 2|B_{\rm ch}|+732|D_{\rm st}|+|E_{\rm tu}|,
\]
on which the error is an arbitrary joint operation; its complement
executes the intended channel. Each marked location is within physical
distance eight of a seed, and its virtual support is in two fixed
role neighborhoods of that seed. At fixed-history operator scope
all exterior coefficients are retained.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Give each vertex its intended pointer \(i(v)\) and actual pointer
\(a(v)\), interpreting NONE as a private idle ID. Mark both IDs for
an erroneous clean vertex; at a dirty station mark every incident port
tuple and its two intended/private-idle IDs; mark every dirty tuple.
Call the set of marked IDs \(\mathcal T\). Let \(\mathcal R\) contain
all erroneous or dirty vertices and every vertex whose intended or
actual pointer is in \(\mathcal T\).

At a correct clean vertex the two pointers coincide. Hence an intended
tuple meeting \(\mathcal R\) has its ID in \(\mathcal T\), so all its
intended operands lie in \(\mathcal R\). This proves intended closure.
An actual tuple receiving retained input from \(\mathcal R\), or
returning an output to it, also has marked ID: at a dirty station all
incident IDs are marked; at an erroneous clean vertex its actual ID is
marked; at a correct clean vertex in \(\mathcal R\) the identical
pointers are marked. Every other retained participant has that same
actual ID and is in \(\mathcal R\). Unselected clean ports are fresh
disposable factors and never gather into an unmarked vertex. A dirty
station touches only \(\mathcal R\) and ports of marked IDs. This is
actual closure.

Every unmarked intended tuple has correct clean pointers, clean ports
and a clean native box. It receives exactly its intended operands,
acts correctly, and returns them. No other selected tuple touches
those operands. Thus the complementary channel is exactly the intended
one even on inputs entangled with \(\mathcal R\) and a reference.
All remaining operations are on the marked union and its work/bath.
Private clean preparation, reset, dephasing and disposal environments
are fresh and never retrieved. Tracing them leaves an arbitrary joint
channel on that union when the fixed history is exogenous. More
generally expand its Stinespring operators coefficientwise, retaining
all exterior systems: tensor matrix bases on disjoint marked operand
sets give precisely the full operator-span interface of
Lemma~\ref{d:reduction}. Individual factors need not be CPTP.
A reset at a marked location is replaced, never inverted.

There are at most \(2|B_{\rm ch}|+(730+2)|D_{\rm st}|+|E_{\rm tu}|\)
marked IDs. Each marked intended location has one of these IDs.
An incident tuple anchor is within two of its port, and its other
operands within four of that station. Enlarging for the adjacent
intended location gives radius eight. In each fixed fold role physical
displacement is bounded, while the two roles can be distant virtually.
This proves the two-neighborhood assertion.
\end{proof}

A whole-site replacement dirties one station, including all local
ports at once. A primitive decomposition error has bounded support
in this fixed catalogue. Time-boundary uses are covered by finitely
many adjacent modules. Iterating Lemma~\ref{f4:dispatch} keeps joint
coefficients on the union of the named locations, exactly as required
by Lemma~\ref{d:reduction}. Repeated metadata uses keep their original
source names.

\begin{lemma}[Unfinished modules]\label{f4:dispatch-partial}
At an elementary time inside a module, retaining actual in-flight
factors and appending its intended suffix gives the same localization
with a station envelope of at most 732 locations per choice-error seed.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Retain semantic qubits, ports, masks holding quantum data and all
undeleted quantum work. Append the fixed intended scatter/native/
gather suffix, with intended choices; do not consult received metadata.
A station whose actual frozen choice differs is conservatively dirty,
so all its incident IDs are marked. At the other stations actual prefix
and intended suffix agree with the reference. The preceding pointer
closure proof applies to their composition. Future reset/disposal is
performed only at its declared instruction, so no in-flight input is
lost. Replacing \(2|B_{\rm ch}|\) by \(732|B_{\rm ch}|\) covers the
whole unfinished station. Use this larger envelope for all choice
seeds when choosing incidence constants, so the estimate is uniform
in the requested readout offset.
\end{proof}

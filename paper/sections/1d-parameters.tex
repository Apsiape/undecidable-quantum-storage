\section{Effective parameter choices}\label{q1:parameters}
This section lists every constant of the ring construction with a constructive bound; Lemma~\ref{q1:l16} shows that they can be chosen in a noncircular order.

The following list gives names and constructive bounds; the order of
choice is given in the proof of Lemma~\ref{q1:l16}. Rank, position, date and code-coordinate indices are variables; distinct named constants in this list remain distinct.

Names in Sections~\ref{q1:model}--\ref{q1:effective} are local to the ring construction. Where a name also occurs in Sections~\ref{sec:controller}--\ref{sec:four-dimensional} or Appendices~\ref{app:code-estimates}--\ref{sec:folded} (\(B\), \(C_h\), \(D_s\), \(K_{\mathrm{cap}}\), \(C_{\mathrm{cap}}\), \(C_{\mathrm{ball}}\), \(C_{\mathrm{raw}}\), \(\Delta_{\mathrm{raw}}\), \(\eps_*\), \(j_*\), \(k_0\), \(N_0\)), it denotes the ring quantity defined here.

\begin{lemma}[No circular dependence and an explicit cone bound]\label{q1:l16}
The choices in the preceding list can be made in the order given in the proof below. Every global constant is fixed before choosing \(J_{\rm pad}\), and one computable \(J_{\rm pad}\) satisfies every required final smallness inequality. In particular, for every \(k\ge0\) the actual elementary dependency cone, over one rank-\(k\) tick (\(H=T_{q}^{k}\) macro rounds, \(D_{\mathrm{layer}}H\) elementary layers), of an aligned rank-\(k\) output home of length \(P_{q}^{k}\), including every actual controller dependency, fits in an interval of length at most
\[
 C_{\rm cone}(T_q^k+P_q^k+1),\qquad
 C_{\rm cone}=16(R_{\rm macro}+P_q+1).
\]
This bound includes all actual elementary controller dependencies and is fixed before the final rate-window selection.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Start with the native quantum roles and port tile, \(K_{\mathrm{inc}},h_{\mathrm{cover}},m_{\mathrm{bad}},b_{\mathrm{fault}}\), the finite CSS code and catalogue. Choose the indexed generator's absolute exponent and its finite self-description. Its arithmetic degree does not depend on the padding exponent. Then the finite R1/quantum timetable inequalities are searches comparing exponential capacities with fixed polynomials, so a single \(w_{q}\) determines \(P_{q},T_{q}\) with \(P_{q}\le T_{q}\) and the whole clean schedule. Native selector geometry is chosen before helper widths, as the explicit parametric native interface proved in Lemma~\ref{q1:l11} states. Choose \(d_{\mathrm{moment}}\) from the now fixed quantum geometry. Choose short exponent, short helper constants, then long exponent and channel count, then the single native \(n_{\mathrm{bin}}\). Choose physical redundancy after those exponents. Now the alphabet, all factories, physical period, slots and spatial shifts are completely fixed. All stencil sizes, clock cutoffs, \(C_{\mathrm{tail}},C_{\mathrm{base}},C_{\mathrm{rec}},C_{\mathrm{top}},C_{\mathrm{path}}\) and \(C_{\mathrm{cone}}\) are computed at this stage, including the cost of idle physical layers.

The helper and exponent inequalities have strictly positive slack powers of \(p\). Their multiplicative constants, and the desired tolerance \(\delta_{\mathrm{mark}}\), therefore require only \(p\) smaller than an effectively found positive threshold. Lemmas~\ref{q1:l1.1} and \ref{q1:l12} give finite-sum and geometric-tail bounds for their infinite moments, so this threshold can be found by rational inequalities with certified tails. Direct moments tend to zero as \(\sqrt{\eps}\). \(\gamma'(0)>0\) supplies fixed upper and lower proportional bounds between \(p_{m}\) and \(\bar\eps_{m}\) near zero. The bin radii tend monotonically to zero superexponentially with \(m\); \(H_{\mathrm{boot}}(m)=D_{\mathrm{boot}}^{m+2}\) is \(p_{m}^{-o(1)}\), with an effective bound from \(A_{\mathrm{bin}}=8^{m+n_{\mathrm{bin}}}\). Every needed sufficiently small inequality thus has a computable uniform tail in \(m\). Enumerate an integer \(J_{\mathrm{pad}}\) whose least \(m_{\mathrm{bin}}\) is beyond that tail. No gate, \(n_{\mathrm{bin}}\), geometry, padding exponent or helper count changes during this last search. In particular the dependence of the clock stencil on \(D_{\mathrm{layer}}\) is settled before the rate threshold is imposed.

For the explicit chart bound, let the decoded output home have length \(P_{q}^{k}\) and let \(H=T_{q}^{k}\). A depth-\(D_{\mathrm{layer}} H\) actual elementary dependency cone can extend spatially by at most \(r_{\mathrm{gate}} D_{\mathrm{layer}} H=R_{\mathrm{macro}} H\) at either end. Rounding the two ends outward to complete aligned rank-\(k\) homes adds less than \(2P_{q}^{k}\). A further fixed one-home reserve and all finitely many initial slot offsets are safely paid by \(C_{\mathrm{cone}}=16(R_{\mathrm{macro}}+P_{q}+1)\): the necessary interval is bounded by \(2R_{\mathrm{macro}} H+4P_{q}^{k}+2\) plus a fixed offset allowance already in \(r_{\mathrm{gate}}\) and \(P_{q}\). This is at most \(C_{\mathrm{cone}}(H+P_{q}^{k}+1)\). Classical controller transitions queried by that output are already included in its actual physical cone; witnesses used solely for probability estimates can live in a separate infinite extension. Thus there is no extra rank-dependent cone constant. The factor 4 in the embedding criterion leaves a strict unused ring margin, so no global ring tiling is needed.

All remaining quantities are per-label finite endpoints or halting-witness quantities. In particular halting-dependent constants are not prerequisites for choosing the physical rule, intervals, encoder or decoder. This proves acyclicity and the claimed computable bounds. 
\end{proof}

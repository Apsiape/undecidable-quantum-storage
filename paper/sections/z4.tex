\section{Stretched-exponential storage in four dimensions}\label{sec:four-dimensional}
The static cap of Section~\ref{sec:exponential} needs two chart
coordinates and two quorum coordinates. A concatenated code uses only
a chain. Combining that cap with Section~\ref{sec:quantum}'s clock
and folding the chain gives the following theorem.

Folding puts the virtual cycle into half its length with two roles per site, so it fits on a physical torus of any side \(L\). We describe the circuit, verify the hierarchy hypotheses and state the source law in Sections~\ref{f4:circuit}--\ref{f4:actual-sources}. Section~\ref{f4:constants} orders the constants, and Sections~\ref{f4:positive} and~\ref{f4:forgetting} prove the storage and forgetting alternatives.

\begin{theorem}[Detailed form of Theorem~\ref{thm:four-dimensional}]\label{f4:detailed}
There is a computably specified, translation-invariant, finite-range,
time-periodic circuit on \(\Z^4\), with a fixed \(N\) qubits per site,
elementary inventory
\(\{X,H,\mathrm{CNOT},\mathrm{Toffoli},R_0,D_Z,I\}\) of arity at
most three, and independent whole-site depolarization
\eqref{eq:site-noise} at every site after every elementary layer.
It acts on all physical factors, including data, clock, controller,
sampling register, port, work, mixed ancilla, reset-following and idle factors. There are
computable pairwise disjoint nonempty rational open intervals \(I_n\)
whose closures lie in \((0,\eps_*)\) for a fixed rational
\(\eps_*>0\), a fixed clock block length \(P_{\rm cal}=2^{r_{\rm exp}}\), \(r_{\rm exp}\ge2\),
\(\alpha_4=1/r_{\rm exp}\), a rational \(c_4>0\) independent of \(n\),
and a total computable threshold \(L_4(n)\), with these properties on
\((\Z/L\Z)^4\) using \eqref{eq:fidelity}.
\begin{enumerate}
\item Total computable maps \(\cE_{n,L},\cD_{n,L,t}\), independent
of \(\eps\) and the halting answer, satisfy
\[
 F(\cE_{n,L},\cD_{n,L,t};L,t,\eps)\ge15/16
 \quad\bigl(U(n)\uparrow,\ \eps\in I_n,\ L\ge L_4(n),\
               0\le t\le\exp(c_4L^{\alpha_4})\bigr).
\]
Time here is an integer number of elementary layers. One encoder
serves every such time and rate.
\item If \(U(n)\downarrow\), then for every \(\eta>0\) a finite
\(C_{n,\eta}\), independent of \(L\) and \(\eps\in I_n\), satisfies
\(F^*_{L,\eps}(t)\le1/2+\eta\) for every \(L\), every such rate,
and every integer \(t\ge C_{n,\eta}\). The supremum includes
arbitrary encodings into the entire physical algebra, arbitrary
decoders, and initial controller, work, intersite and reference
entanglement.
\end{enumerate}
\end{theorem}

The windows decrease on the \(4^{-n}\) scale. The exponent \(\alpha_4<2\times10^{-4}\) is small. The crossover with a linear lower bound depends on the coefficients and size cutoffs (Section~\ref{intro:scope}).

\subsection{The four-dimensional circuit}\label{f4:circuit}
Write coordinates \((x,y,\sigma,z)\). The cap field \(\mathsf C\)
is exactly the core, ordinary second proposal, base sampling registers, deep
queries and availability dictionary of Section~\ref{sec:exponential},
with chart plane \((x,y)\), quorum plane \((\sigma,z)\), and the
copied coordinates \(P_3,P_4\) deleted. In particular the static
constraints are put in nearest-neighbour higher-block form before
adding the counter. Its Moore rule retains the original profile, spreads
bottom from every input, and spreads death along original-active
nearest-neighbour edges. Death leaves the cap boundary and endpoints
unchanged. The first coordinate enables storage exactly when its retained profile is active and its death flag \(\mathsf d\) is unset. The second records the ordinary majority proposal. Only the two depth-4096 profile queries \(Q_0,Q_-\) can provide harmful guards. The availability dictionary distinguishes bottom from vacuum: the former writes unknown/allowed, the latter unknown/unavailable. A genuine row origin takes priority over predecessor vacuum. Every shifted sampling register uses its own base site.

An independent clock field \(\mathsf S\) runs along \(\sigma\),
with whole-symbol quorum protection in \((x,z)\), cloned in \(y\)
in the reference. Its logical rule is the transducer of
Lemma~\ref{f4:periodic}, total on malformed fields, folded into two
roles by the coordinate map of Lemma~\ref{f4:dispatch-clean}. Endpoint
flags compute virtual predecessor and successor by physical neighbours
and on-site role switches, so the folded stencil has radius at most two,
independently of the code and the clock alphabet. Boundary/parity
fields are retained by the logical rule. Its stored current request,
finite boundary flags and private old copies supply the exclusive
selectors of Appendix~\ref{app:f4-dispatch}.
The patch and clock evolve autonomously: neither reads the other or the quantum data, and the clock also ignores the sampling registers. They can share a physical noise event.

There are two semantic quantum factors and at most 730 port factors
per site, plus one mixed cut ancilla per persistent target and a fixed
finite amount of temporary work. Additional code and syndrome qubits
occupy more virtual chain cells. Every quantum-derived record remains
quantum-capable. The classical alphabet and its Boolean work become
fixed finite registers after the constants are selected.

A round has the following fixed order. Dephase and make one-way private
old metadata copies; compute and freeze finite exclusive selectors.
Execute scatter, unconditional native tuples and gather, including
padded idles. Then compute next cap and clock by destination-local
Boolean work on transported old inputs, recomputing provisional base
gathers locally. Apply the conditional reset controlled by the first coordinate at each physical site
to every retained quantum-capable factor. Its private mixed ancilla
is reset/discarded after use and never returned. Temporary factors
either cross the cut or terminate at their unconditional reset before
reuse. After the cut only on-site fresh preparation, disposal and
identity/noise padding remain. Every factor belongs to autonomous
dephased metadata or to this quantum inventory.

Section~\ref{sec:compilation} and Appendix~\ref{app:f4-dispatch}
compile this rule into the seven elementary gates. Long Boolean
computations are local to their destination; only fixed old transports
precede them. Quantum spatial motion is confined to \(\sigma\) at
fixed \((x,y,z)\), even for malformed metadata. Private internal ports
make translated tuples disjoint also on small tori. The range of deep
quorum queries and sampling-register transport is large but fixed. The period,
range, site dimension and rule read no size, input index or rate.

\subsection{Complete quantum geometry}\label{f4:geometry-section}
\begin{lemma}[Folded complete-child reduction]\label{f4:geometry}
The circuit has code-independent incidence and child-index halo
constants chosen before the code. With the code and padding chosen
below, its regular hierarchy satisfies the complete-child hypotheses
of Lemma~\ref{f4:recursion} and admits the fixed joint recovery of
Lemma~\ref{d:reduction}.
\end{lemma}
\par\noindent\emph{Proof idea.} Use the full port support of a faulty local choice, including work still in flight. Match these supports to the child cover of the folded chain and bound the separation needed for level reduction.

\begin{proof}
Fix the primitive port/native/cut catalogue first, padding inactive
candidates by their intended idle reference. For each wrong gate choice, include the stations that can still be in use by an unfinished module. Lemmas~\ref{f4:dispatch}
and~\ref{f4:dispatch-partial} put each dirty location in two fixed
role neighbourhoods of radius eight, with arbitrary joint coefficients.
Let \(h_{\rm base}\) cover these footprints and the one-child-step ownership
segment at the immediately following common cut. This segment is
independent of the code; the full normalizer circuit belongs to its
scheduled rectangle. Let \(\kappa_0\) bound station-to-location
conversion, \(\kappa_1\) child cell-time participation, and \(v_0\)
parent boxes meeting a rectangle. These are maxima on that fixed
local catalogue. More ancillas use more cells and repeated EC uses
more times, without increasing participation at one cell-time.

A level-\(\ell\) regular box has length \(P_{\rm cal}^\ell\), time height
\(T_{\rm cal}^\ell\), and its \(P_{\rm cal}\)-by-\(T_{\rm cal}\) child cover plus \(h_{\rm base}\)
child-index layers. Geometric series bound every complete descendant
by \mbox{\(h_{\rm halo}=2h_{\rm base}+2\)}, enlarging once for rectangle covers. Choose
\[
 \rho_{\rm gap}\ge1,\qquad
 a_{\rm sep}>(4h_{\rm halo}+2\lceil\rho_{\rm gap}\rceil+5)^2,\qquad
 b_{\rm FT}=\max(1,\kappa_0\kappa_1v_0(a_{\rm sep}-1)).
\]
Use \(r_{\rm FT}=8b_{\rm FT}\), and the least code
parameter with \(q_{\rm code}=2^{s_{\rm code}}-1
\ge40\,960(b_{\rm FT}+1)\). Lemmas~\ref{d:code}--\ref{d:gadgets}
apply to these exact native inputs. Precede each complete body by
\(b_{\rm FT}+1\) time-disjoint full EC passes with private work,
synchronized before any interblock body; idle blocks normalize too.
If the at most \(v_0\) covering parent boxes are good, they contain
at most \(v_0(a_{\rm sep}-1)\) bad regular children, marking at most
\(b_{\rm FT}\) child circuit locations. The last clean EC gives
code support, and all subsequent passes and the body satisfy
\[
 8(r_{\rm FT}+b_{\rm FT})=72b_{\rm FT}\le q_{\rm code}-8.
\]
The operator proof of Lemma~\ref{d:reduction} preserves all
good logicals jointly. Absorb outgoing maps only on the union of bad
outgoing wires before the next body; retain all shared coefficients.
This proves deterministic reduction at each level.

A bad parent has at least \(a_{\rm sep}\) bad children. At most
\((4h_{\rm halo}+2\lceil\rho_{\rm gap}\rceil+5)^2\) complete child halos are
within \(\rho_{\rm gap}P_{\rm cal}^\ell\) of one full halo in both position
and time; here \(T_{\rm cal}^\ell\ge P_{\rm cal}^\ell\). Hence a separated pair exists.
There are finitely many pairs after \(P_{\rm cal},T_{\rm cal}\) are fixed, and root count
is \(O(P_{\rm cal}^{5\ell})\) since \(T_{\rm cal}=P_{\rm cal}^4\). Choose the fixed real table
first and then a sufficiently large power of two \(P_{\rm cal}\) so its routed
work and interpreter fit, as in Lemma~\ref{f4:periodic}. Choose even
\(s_{\rm top}\) larger than every complete spatial halo. On
\(m=s_{\rm top}P_{\rm cal}^k\) all relevant supports are proper cycle arcs.
The exact periodic reference supplies the repeated encoded identity
at every aligned time.

Only the active folded cycle is the code layout. The extension
\(a_r\) outside the interval is a source image. Other physical
factors are exterior systems. If a faulty tuple couples them to active
data, first use closed-support localization, then expand in matrix
bases on its marked active operands, retaining exterior coefficients.
Their number and source stars are unchanged. This is exactly the
operator scope corrected by the common recovery.
\end{proof}

\subsection{The source law}\label{f4:actual-sources}
\begin{lemma}[Four-dimensional source interface]\label{f4:sources}
The initialized nonhalting circuit satisfies all actual-source inputs
of Appendix~\ref{app:f4-sources}, with constants independent of the
window. Its raw exceptional probability through \(T_{\rm hor}\) is at most
\(C_{\rm bd}(T_{\rm hor}+C_{\rm bd})L^4e^{-\beta_{\rm raw}L}\), and its cap exceptional probability
is at most \(C_{\rm bd}(T_{\rm hor}+C_{\rm bd})L^4e^{-\beta_{\rm cap} L/(w_n+1)}\).
\end{lemma}
\par\noindent\emph{Proof idea.} Project the six-dimensional controller proof by deleting its copied coordinates. The clock and the controller then use their separate source estimates, with the dispatch contribution counted in the combined graph.

\begin{proof}
Deleting the copied \(P_3,P_4\) coordinates changes none of the cap's
Moore, replica, base, deep-query or sampling-register increments. The complete
ordinary and statistical arguments of Section~\ref{v:parameters}
therefore apply with the same two quorum coordinates. At large enough
\(L\), one cap square, its vacuum gap and every \(W\)-experiment
have injective support hulls; no divisibility of \(L\) is required.
At a fixed time the characteristic map is bijective, and distinct
integer bins have disjoint times. Thus distinct cap raw names use
disjoint physical flag sets. Statistical requests use the three bin
residues and private base ownership; overlay events use complete-bin
support colours. The proof of \eqref{v:joint} gives
\[
 \Pr(X_\lambda\text{ for all }\lambda\in F)\le\gamma_{\rm src}^{|F|},\qquad
 \gamma_{\rm src}=\sqrt{2C_h}+\eta_+(B)^{1/6}
                      +\alpha_{\rm bin}^{1/(2D_{\rm bin})}.
\]
Here \(C_h\) is the sampling-register scale, with the tails in \eqref{v:etas} evaluated at the chosen scale (see Table~\ref{tab:concordance}). Added clock operations only
enlarge the internal overlay at that site and round. Their private
roles do not alter base ownership or introduce cap inputs.

For completeness the witness calculation before the sampling-register count is as
follows. On each charge arm every clean deep entry owns all
\(D_{\rm q}=4096\) support occurrences before returning to an actual
old word. If \(e\) is the support count, \(m_{\rm ent}\) the entry count and \(g\)
distinct lifted terminal groups, then
\[
 4096m_{\rm ent}\le e,\qquad129e-1440(g-1)-696m_{\rm ent}\le\Omega_{\rm pre}g.
\]
Shared suffixes are counted before projection; faulty evaluation ends
at an overlay terminal. The inequality \(129-696/4096>128\) gives
\(e\le(1440+\Omega_{\rm pre})g/128\). Sampling-register rides keep their base
characteristic and have bounded total bin increment. Keep their two
endpoint bins and compress them into a typed corridor. An incorrect censor without a hardware fault is an intrinsic or overlay exception at its source label, or traces back to one of the two base queries. Receiver failures and all
\(B\) lane faults are aggregated in the original overlay source label, so
there is no sampling-register index decoration. Entries/corridors add at most two
links per support plus fixed source stars. Thus complete lifted
witnesses have \(E\le c_Cg\), bounded degree and anchored exponential
entropy, with the explicit catalogue bounds in Section~\ref{f4:order-a}.
All five characteristic coordinates and finite roles remain in that
graph. Initial availability is unavailable, and sampling registers start by their
own base: a recursion reaching time zero without a source is correct.
Actual inputs move backward in physical time or in a bounded virtual
forecast depth. Every retained source and endpoint bin is consequently
in the finite causal range specified in Appendix~\ref{app:f4-sources}.

For the clock, whole-symbol prediction uses only its fixed logical
\(\sigma\)-stencil and the replica boxes in \((x,z)\). Wrong clean
outputs require wrong predictions in two boxes. The three potentials in
Lemma~\ref{f4:raw} ignore the logical \(\sigma\) and copied \(y\)
shifts, so their gains remain \(132,129,129\). One-way old transport
makes the raw cause/use multiplicity depend only on this stencil,
not the alphabet or lookup rows. Apply Lemma~\ref{f4:raw} in \(d=4\),
including boundary, opcode, old/new and current-copy errors.
Direct dispatcher hardware uses the same physical raw source label.

Cap errors explain wrong conditional resets; raw clock errors explain wrong
choices and malformed endpoints. Attach the exact current-use stars
of Lemma~\ref{f4:dispatch-partial} before selecting the central-strip
cutoff. Lemmas~\ref{f4:strip}--\ref{f4:mixed} now apply with
\(w=w_n\). They give one source image and the fourth-root joint law
for all short certificates. Source vertices outside the active code
remain in full witness entropy with their existing names. Only active
quantum roots are glued to the hierarchy. The two exceptional bounds
follow directly, under the original physical law.
\end{proof}
